# Get an MDRRMO certification or report

If the MDRRMO attended to you, you can ask it for a **certification or report** to claim insurance or for other
legal purposes. It is **free** and takes about **30 minutes**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

---

## Where

|           |                                                                  |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Office    | Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO) |
| Address   | Aguja St., Barangay Baybay, Carigara                             |
| Phone     | (053) 888 2895 · 0949 860 8899 · 0927 908 6881                   |
| Email     | ldrrmocarigaraleyte@gmail.com                                    |
| Signed by | Mr. Alejo N. Urmeneta, MDRRM Officer                             |
| Fee       | **None**                                                         |

**Who can ask:** anyone the MDRRMO attended to, including businesses.

## What to bring

- A **request letter** or order — one original
- A **company or agency ID**, or another related document, as proof

## Steps

| You                                                   | The MDRRMO                                                                      | Time       |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Sign the visitor's logbook and hand in your letter | Records it and passes it to the officer in charge for checking                  | 5 minutes  |
| 2. Wait for approval                                  | Works out what kind of document you need; the MDRRM Officer checks and signs it | 20 minutes |
| 3. Sign the logbook and receive the document          | Records and releases the certification                                          | 5 minutes  |

**Total: 30 minutes.** No fees.
