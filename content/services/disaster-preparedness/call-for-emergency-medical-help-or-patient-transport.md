# Call for emergency medical help or patient transport

**Emergency? Call the MDRRMO: (053) 888 2895 · 0949 860 8899 · 0927 908 6881.**
The office runs **24/7** and the service is **free**. For a small-scale
incident, the charter sets a maximum of **45 minutes** in the poblacion and
along national roads, or **1 hour 30 minutes** in interior and upland
barangays — that is the whole response, not a promised arrival time.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## In an emergency

For serious illness or injury. Nothing to bring.

| You                                                       | The MDRRMO                                                                                         | Time                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 1. Call, or go to the MDRRMO office                       | Takes your name, number and what happened, checks it, and passes it to the emergency response team | 5 minutes                        |
| 2. Wait for the team                                      | Sends a rescue vehicle or ambulance; calls the police if needed                                    | Depends on distance              |
| 3. The team treats the patient and takes them to hospital | Gives first aid and hands the patient over to a hospital or other facility                         | Depends on the injury or illness |

**When you call, say:** your name, a number to call back on, and what
happened and where.

**Maximum response time, start to hand-over:**

| Where the incident is              | Maximum               |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Poblacion and along national roads | **45 minutes**        |
| Interior and upland barangays      | **1 hour 30 minutes** |

The charter gives these for "small-scale incidents".

## Patient transport to a hospital

For **residents of Carigara** who need to be taken to a higher-level hospital
and the **Municipal Health Office ambulance is not available**. The MDRRMO
vehicle is used if it is free on the date and time you ask for. Emergencies
always come first.

### What to bring

- A **Two-Way Health Referral Form**, signed by a doctor, if possible — one
  original and one photocopy. You get it from the hospital or health facility
  that referred you.
- **Other supporting documents**, such as a laboratory request form — one
  original and one photocopy

### Steps

| You                                                         | The MDRRMO                                                                                       | Time      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------- |
| 1. Sign the visitor's logbook and hand in the referral form | Checks the form, interviews you, and makes sure the receiving hospital has been coordinated with | 3 minutes |
| 2. Wait for approval                                        | Checks that a vehicle and staff are free, prepares the trip papers and sends the team            | 3 minutes |
| 3. The trip                                                 | Monitors the patient's vital signs on the way and hands them over to the hospital                | See below |

| To                    | Maximum, including hand-over |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Palo or Tacloban City | **1 hour 40 minutes**        |
| Ormoc City            | **1 hour 55 minutes**        |

Waiting time at the hospital during hand-over is not counted.
