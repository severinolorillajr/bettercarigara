# Ask the MDRRMO to run a disaster training or drill

Residents, businesses and government offices can ask the MDRRMO to send
trainers for disaster-related **trainings, seminars, orientations, emergency
drills and simulation exercises**. Send a letter **at least 5 to 7 working
days** before the date you want. It is **free**, and the office confirms the
schedule in about **4 days**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

---

| Fee     | **None**                                                         |

**Who can ask:** anyone.

## What to bring

- A **request letter** addressed to the **Municipal Mayor**, with attention to
  the **MDRRM Officer**. Submit it **at least 5 to 7 working days** before the
  date you want.

## Steps

| You                                                   | The MDRRMO                                                                                                  | Time                |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| 1. Sign the visitor's logbook and hand in your letter | Acknowledges it and passes it to the officer in charge of training                                          | 5 minutes           |
| 2. Wait for approval                                  | The MDRRM Officer reviews the request, chooses the topics and trainers, and prepares the training materials | 2 to 3 working days |
| 3. Receive the confirmed schedule                     | Tells you the approved date and holds a coordination meeting with you                                       | 1 day               |

**Total: up to 4 days and 5 minutes.** No fees.

The charter's wording on timing is unclear: the letter is due 5 to 7 working
days ahead "provided there were previous scheduled activity on the same date".
If your date is already taken by another activity, ask the office.

- Which topics and drills are offered

- How residents can take part in a barangay's own evacuation planning
