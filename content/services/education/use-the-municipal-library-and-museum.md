# Use the Municipal Library and Museum

The Carigara Municipal Library and Museum is on Aguja St., Barangay Baybay.
Reading inside the library and borrowing books for home are **free** — bring a
valid ID. A visit to read takes about **15 minutes** of registration and
check-out; borrowing about **14 minutes**. You can request a museum and library
tour by **request letter**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## What to bring

Any one of these:

- A **library card** — from the Carigara Municipal Library
- A **student ID**
- A **company ID**
- A **government-issued ID** — SSS, GSIS, Pag-IBIG, postal ID or similar

## Reading in the library

| You                                                                       | The library                                           | Time      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Write your details in the logbook at the entrance desk                 | Monitors registration                                 | 2 minutes |
| 2. Leave your bag at the baggage counter — keep phone, cash and valuables | Gives you a baggage number                            | 2 minutes |
| 3. Ask the librarian or staff for what you need                           | Helps you find the books or sources                   | 3 minutes |
| 4. Fill out the book card and hand it over with your ID                   | Clips your ID to the book card and gives you the book | 5 minutes |
| 5. When done, wait for your record to be cleared                          | Clears the book card and returns your ID              | 3 minutes |

The library says it is **not liable for losses** from the baggage counter.

## Borrowing books for home

| You                                                      | The library                                                            | Time      |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Present your ID; first-timers fill out a library card | Scans your ID                                                          | 1 minute  |
| 2. Fill out the book card                                | Checks the book, stamps the due date, and files your ID with the cards | 5 minutes |
| 3. Return the books                                      | Receives them and charges fines for overdue or lost books              | 3 minutes |
| 4. Wait for your record to be cleared                    | Clears your record and returns your ID                                 | 5 minutes |

**Fines are paid in kind**, not cash: the charter says in the form of book
donations, acetate, floor wax or other donations, for which the library issues
a receipt. It lists the fee for borrowing as **none**.

## Museum and library tours

Tours showcase history, culture and art through curated exhibits.

1. **Write a request letter** addressed to the Municipal Mayor or Vice Mayor,
   with a copy to the Municipal Librarian. Drop it off at the **Mayor's
   Office**, the **Sangguniang Bayan Office** and the **Library and Museum**.
   It is recorded, assessed and recommended for approval — about **10
   minutes**.
2. **Follow up** by phone or Facebook Messenger. The librarian coordinates the
   status and the final arrangements — about **30 minutes**.

| Fee  | Amount   |
| ---- | -------- |
| Tour | **None** |
