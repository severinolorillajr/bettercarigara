# Enroll Children in LGU Daycare or Preschool Programs

_Not yet published for Carigara._

This page will explain how to access this service in Carigara, Leyte — the
requirements, the steps, where to go, and who to contact.

Nothing is published here yet because the starter kit shipped this page with
another city's information, and guessing at Carigara's equivalent would be worse
than leaving it blank. Every detail added here needs a public source.

**Where to source it:** DepEd Leyte Division, CHED Region VIII, DOST-SEI, OWWA, and Municipal Hall scholarship postings.
