# Apply for an internship or OJT

Students can do their **on-the-job training (OJT)** at the municipality through
the Human Resource Management Office — senior high school students through
their school, college students on their own with a school endorsement. Young
residents can also be referred to the **Government Internship Program (GIP)**,
and students or out-of-school youth aged **15 to 30** can apply for
**SPES**, about **20 working days** of paid work, both through the Public
Employment Service Office (PESO). None of these has a fee.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## OJT for senior high school students

Your **school** applies on your behalf; the municipality and the school sign a
Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) first.

**What is needed:** a letter of intent from the school principal or head; the
signed MOA between the municipality and the school; an endorsement letter
from the school listing the interns, with their resumes and latest photos;
and your daily time record.

| You / your school                                                     | The office                                                                 | Time       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. The school's OJT coordinator submits a letter of intent            | Answers questions; once accepted, sends an electronic copy of the MOA      | 5 minutes  |
| 2. The school prints, signs and notarises the MOA, and submits it     | Asks for the endorsement letter and résumés, and sets the orientation date | 15 minutes |
| 3. Students attend the OJT orientation on the start date              | Explains the rules and sends each intern to an assigned office             | 1 hour     |
| 4. After your required hours, submit your accomplishment rating sheet | Checks your hours and issues a **certificate of completion**               | 30 minutes |

Total: **1 hour and 50 minutes** of processing. The number of hours you must
complete is set by your school.

## OJT for college students

You apply yourself, at the **Office of the Mayor or the HRMO**.

**What to bring:** an endorsement letter from your school OJT coordinator or
dean, and your resume with a recent photo. You keep a daily time record during
the OJT.

| You                                                                   | The office                                                   | Time       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| 1. Submit your endorsement letter and resume                          | Reviews them and tells you when the internship starts        | 15 minutes |
| 2. Attend the OJT orientation on the start date                       | Explains the rules and sends you to an assigned office       | 1 hour     |
| 3. After your required hours, submit your accomplishment rating sheet | Checks your hours and issues a **certificate of completion** | 30 minutes |

Total: **1 hour and 45 minutes**.

## Government Internship Program (GIP)

A programme under Executive Order No. 139 (1993) for young workers,
"particularly the poor/indigent", to work in government. PESO's part is to screen you
and **refer you to DOLE** (the charter says "DOLE – NLFO"). **Residents
only.**

**What to bring:**

- GIP application form and National Skills Registration Program (NSRP) form —
  both from PESO
- Certificate of indigency and certificate of residency — from your barangay
  hall
- Transcript of Records or certificate of graduation — from your school
- A valid ID, your resume or PDS, and **two 1×1 photos**

| You                         | The office                                      | Time       |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Fill out the NSRP form   | Receives it                                     | 10 minutes |
| 2. Submit your requirements | The PESO Manager gives you an initial interview | 30 minutes |
| —                           | Refers you to DOLE with a referral letter       | 30 minutes |

Total: **1 hour and 10 minutes**.

## SPES — paid work for students

The **Special Program for the Employment of Students** gives temporary work to
"disadvantaged/poor but deserving" students and out-of-school youth so they
can keep studying.

**Who can apply:**

- Students or out-of-school youth (OSY) aged **15 to 30**. OSY must be
  certified as such by their barangay.
- Your parents' combined net income, plus your own, must not exceed the
  regional poverty threshold.
- Students must have a passing general weighted average.

**What to bring:**

- SPES application form — from PESO
- PSA birth certificate
- Previous and current grades — from your school
- Income tax return
- Certificate of indigency — from your barangay
- Certification of out-of-school youth (OSY only) — from your barangay

| You                                                    | The office                                                              | Time             |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| 1. Submit your requirements and have a short interview | Checks the documents; staff and the PESO Manager interview you          | 40 minutes       |
| 2. Wait for word **within 5 days**                     | Tells you whether you are approved and when the orientation is          | 5 days           |
| 3. Attend the orientation and sign your contract       | Explains the rules, then sends you to an office for **20 working days** | 2 hours          |
| 4. Submit your DTR and accomplishment report           | Verifies them and prepares the payroll                                  | 1 day 30 minutes |

Total: **6 days, 3 hours and 10 minutes** of processing, not counting the 20
working days of work.
