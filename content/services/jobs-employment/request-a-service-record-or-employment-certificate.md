# Request a service record or employment certificate

If you used to work for the municipality — retired, resigned or otherwise
separated — the Human Resource Management Office (HRMO) can issue your
**service record** or a **certificate of employment** for any legal purpose.
Send a written request; it takes about **3 days** and costs **nothing**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

---

## Where

|         |                                                                        |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Office  | Human Resource Management Office (HRMO)                                |
| Address | 2nd floor, Municipal Hall, E. Makabenta St., Barangay Ponong, Carigara |
| Phone   | 0969 580 0427                                                          |
| Email   | hrmocarigara@gmail.com                                                 |
| Head    | Mrs. Liza N. Pernis, Officer-in-Charge                                 |
| Fee     | **None**                                                               |

**Who can ask:** former and incumbent officials and employees.

## What to bring

- A **written request** saying which document you need and what it is for
- If someone goes for you: an **authorisation letter**, with photocopies of
  **your valid ID and theirs**

## Steps

| You                                                    | The office                                               | Time      |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Submit your letter request                          | Receives and reviews it                                  | 5 minutes |
| 2. Wait while the document is prepared                 | Checks your employment records and prepares the document | 3 days    |
| —                                                      | The HRMO head reviews and signs it                       | 5 minutes |
| 3. Receive it and sign the logbook and the HRMO's copy | Releases the document and records it                     | 5 minutes |

Total: **3 days and 15 minutes**.
