# Apply for a job at the municipality

The municipal government hires in three ways: **job order** (a contract of
about **6 months**), **casual** (a temporary appointment of **up to one
year**) and **plantilla** (a regular position). All three go through the Human
Resource Management Office (HRMO), none has a fee, and the charter puts the
process at about **5 days** for job order and casual and **17 days** for
plantilla, most of it a 15-day posting period.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## The three kinds of job

|                 | Job order                                              | Casual                                                                             | Plantilla                                                                |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| What it is      | A contract for about 6 months, or as long as needed    | A temporary appointment for essential work when there are not enough regular staff | A regular post, published through the Civil Service Commission           |
| How long        | About 6 months                                         | **No more than one year**                                                          | Regular                                                                  |
| How it starts   | You apply at the HRMO                                  | The office that needs you sends the HRMO an endorsement letter                     | You apply for a published vacancy                                        |
| Who decides     | HRMO, then the head of the department with the vacancy | The Mayor signs the appointment                                                    | The Promotion and Selection Board; the Civil Service Commission approves |
| Processing time | **5 days**                                             | **5 days and 3 minutes**                                                           | **17 days and 54 minutes**, including a **15-day** posting period        |

## What to bring

Job order asks mostly for photocopies and membership numbers. Casual and
plantilla ask for authenticated documents and a full set of clearances.

| Document                                                           | Job order                | Casual        | Plantilla     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ | ------------- | ------------- |
| Application letter to the Municipal Mayor naming the position      | —                        | Yes           | Yes           |
| Personal Data Sheet with latest photo and signature ¹              | Yes                      | Yes           | Yes           |
| Transcript of Records                                              | Photocopy, if applicable | Authenticated | Authenticated |
| Diploma                                                            | Photocopy, if applicable | Authenticated | Authenticated |
| Civil Service or PRC eligibility, if necessary                     | —                        | Authenticated | Authenticated |
| Latest IPCR (performance rating)                                   | —                        | Photocopy     | Original      |
| PSA birth certificate (photocopy)                                  | Yes                      | —             | —             |
| PSA marriage certificate (original)                                | —                        | Yes           | Yes           |
| Verified SSS, Pag-IBIG and PhilHealth numbers                      | Yes                      | —             | —             |
| Medical certificate and drug test, original ²                      | Yes                      | Yes           | Yes           |
| Barangay clearance and police clearance, original                  | Yes                      | Yes           | Yes           |
| NBI clearance, original                                            | —                        | Yes           | Yes           |
| Mayor's, MTC Branch 13, RTC Branch 36 and Prosecutor's clearances  | —                        | Yes           | Yes           |
| BIR Contract of Service registration (Form 1901 and/or 1905), copy | Yes                      | —             | —             |

¹ For casual and plantilla, the charter specifies CSC Form 212, downloadable
at www.csc.gov.ph.
² From a government health facility or a private clinic.

The charter lists the IPCR and the marriage certificate without saying who
they apply to. The IPCR is a performance rating that a first-time applicant
would not have; ask the HRMO.

If you are hired into a casual or plantilla post, the HRMO prepares the
appointment papers — appointment, oath of office, assumption of duty and
certificate of availability of funds — for you to sign.

## Job order — steps

| You                                                           | The office                                                                        | Time       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Write your details in the applicants' logbook              | Hands you the logbook                                                             | 2 minutes  |
| 2. Submit your PDS and documents                              | Stamps them received, reviews them, and gives you an initial interview            | 32 minutes |
| 3. If you pass and there is an opening, see the HRMO head     | Endorses you to the head of the department with the vacancy for a final interview | 47 minutes |
| 4. If you pass, go to the Mayor's Office                      | Issues an accommodation slip                                                      | 5 minutes  |
| 5. Complete the pre-employment requirements                   | HRMO tells you what is still needed. You have **5 working days**                  | 3 days     |
| 6. Submit them                                                | Checks them and issues your job order contract                                    | 20 minutes |
| 7. Route it for signatures, have it notarised, return it      | Checks that it is complete                                                        | 3 minutes  |
| 8. Submit your processed BIR Contract of Service registration | Checks it and sends you to your assigned office for orientation                   | 8 minutes  |

The charter's total is **5 days**, though its steps add up to about 3 days and
2 hours.

## Plantilla — steps

| You                                                              | The office                                                                      | Time       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Check the published vacancies and whether you qualify         | Publishes them for **15 days** online and in three conspicuous places           | 15 days    |
| 2. Submit your complete requirements to the HRMO                 | Checks completeness and qualifications, and tells you whether you passed        | 4 minutes  |
| 3. Wait for a Notice of Screening by email                       | Schedules the screening once the application period ends                        | 5 minutes  |
| 4. Go to the Municipal Hall on the date in the notice            | The Promotion and Selection Board interviews and assesses you                   | 10 minutes |
| 5. If found qualified, report to the HRMO with your requirements | Gathers the requirements and tells you your official date of assumption         | 2 days     |
| 6. Sign your appointment papers and take your oath               | Prepares them, administers the oath, and sends them to CSC Leyte Field Office I | 35 minutes |

You are told your average rating "in a few days" after the interview.

The charter says the vacancies are listed at **www.csc.weebly.com**. That is
the address as printed. It is not the Civil Service Commission's own website,
and this site has not checked what it shows.

## Casual — steps

| You                                                  | The office                                                                             | Time              |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| —                                                    | Receives an endorsement letter from the office hiring you                              | 1 minute          |
| 1. Submit your complete requirements to the HRMO     | Receives them and prints your appointment papers                                       | 17 minutes        |
| 2. Sign the appointment, oath and assumption of duty | Sends them to the Mayor, and to the Municipal Accountant for the availability of funds | 4 days 15 minutes |
| 3. Take your oath of office                          | Administers it and sends the papers to CSC Leyte Field Office I                        | 30 minutes        |

The charter's total is **5 days and 3 minutes**, though its steps add up to 4
days, 1 hour and 3 minutes.
