# Get help as an OFW

The **OFW Help Desk** at the Municipal Hall writes endorsements and
recommendations for overseas Filipino workers, former OFWs and their
beneficiaries — for example for **repatriation** or for programmes of
**OWWA** (the Overseas Workers Welfare Administration) and the **DMW** (the
Department of Migrant Workers). It takes about **20 minutes** and costs
**nothing**; the help itself comes from OWWA or the DMW.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

---

**Who can ask:** OFWs, former OFWs and OFW beneficiaries.

## What to bring

- A **letter of intent or request** saying what you need
- A **barangay clearance or certification** that the barangay chairman knows
  you personally, if applicable
- The OFW's **passport**
- The OFW's **plane ticket or exit visa**

## Steps

| You                                             | The office                                                              | Time       |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Present your letter and supporting documents | Reviews them                                                            | 5 minutes  |
| —                                               | Endorses you to the OWWA Family Welfare Officer for assessment and help | 15 minutes |
| 2. Wait for word from OWWA or the DMW           | Tells you to expect feedback from them on your request                  | —          |

Total: **20 minutes** at the help desk.
