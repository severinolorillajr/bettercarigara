# Find a job through PESO

The Public Employment Service Office (PESO) registers job seekers and matches
them with employers' vacancies. If there is a match, you leave with a **job
referral letter** in about **1 hour and 20 minutes**, for free. PESO also runs
job fairs and recruitment activities, and the Mayor's Office can issue a
**recommendation for employment** in about **17 minutes**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Job referral and placement

For **residents only**. PESO directs pre-screened job seekers to employers
with matching vacancies; "placement" is when the referral leads to a job.

**What to bring:** your biodata, resume or Personal Data Sheet. PESO provides
the referral logbook and the referral letter.

| You                                        | The office                                                                          | Time       |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Fill out the NSRP registration form     | Receives it                                                                         | 20 minutes |
| 2. Submit your requirements                | Interviews you and encodes your details in the Public Employment Information System | 20 minutes |
| 3. Job matching                            | Checks the vacancies against your qualifications                                    | 20 minutes |
| —                                          | The PESO Manager releases your **job referral letter**                              | 10 minutes |
| 4. Go to the employer you were referred to | Asks you to sign the logbook and fill out a feedback form                           | 10 minutes |

Total: **1 hour and 20 minutes**.

The NSRP form is the National Skills Registration Program form. Your
registration stays on file: PESO lets accredited employers browse the forms of
walk-in job seekers.

## Job fairs

A job fair brings job seekers and employers or overseas recruitment agencies
to one place on one date. It is open to the unemployed, skilled and unskilled
workers, fresh graduates, graduates of training institutions, displaced
workers and employees seeking advancement. You choose vacancies that fit your
qualifications, and employers can interview and **hire on the spot**.

The charter lists the process only for companies taking part. It does not
publish job fair dates.

## Local and special recruitment activities

PESO also hosts recruitment by a single company:

- A **Local Recruitment Activity (LRA)** is for one local company, "without
  any fee".
- A **Special Recruitment Activity (SRA)** is for an overseas agency recruiting
  workers to deploy abroad. The agency needs a Special Recruitment Authority
  from the Department of Migrant Workers and a No Objection Certificate from
  the municipality.

Vacancies for these activities are posted on the PESO Facebook page.

## Career guidance for Grade 10

PESO's **Career Development Support Program** holds a Career Day for Grade 10
students of Carigara's five secondary schools. Schools request it by letter;
the charter puts the whole process at **5 days and 30 minutes**.

## Livelihood and skills training

DOLE livelihood assistance, TUPAD emergency employment and referral to skills
training are also handled by PESO. See
[Join livelihood or skills training programs](/services/social-welfare/join-livelihood-or-skills-training-programs).

## For employers

Employers can, at no charge in the charter:

- **Post job vacancies** — on the PESO bulletin board and Facebook page, in
  about **1 hour and 10 minutes**
- **Browse applicants** — the NSRP forms of registered job seekers, in about
  **1 hour**
- **Hold an LRA or SRA** — about **5 days, 1 hour and 20 minutes**; send a
  letter of intent to the Mayor through the PESO Manager, by walk-in or to
  carigara.peso@gmail.com
- **Join a job fair** — tell PESO by email; about **40 minutes**

For posting and browsing, bring a company profile, business documents, a DOLE
certificate, your current vacancies, a letter of intent (for posting,
addressed to the Mayor through the PESO Manager) and, for overseas jobs, a
POEA licence. LRAs, SRAs and job fairs have longer lists (business permit,
BIR, SEC and DTI registration, DOLE accreditation and others); ask PESO for
the current checklist.
