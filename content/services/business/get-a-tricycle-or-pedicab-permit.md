# Get a tricycle or pedicab permit

To run a tricycle for hire in Carigara you need a **Motorized Tricycle
Operator's Permit (MTOP)**, valid for **one year** and renewed every year. A
pedicab needs its own **pedicab permit**, also valid for **one year**, which
costs **₱695** in total. Both are paid at the Municipal Treasurer's Office, in
about **15 minutes** at the counter. If you stop using a tricycle for hire, you
apply for a **dropping order**, which costs **₱100**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Tricycle: the MTOP

The MTOP is the franchise that lets a tricycle ply and operate within
Carigara. It is valid for **one year from the date it is issued**.

### What to bring

- **Community Tax Certificate** (cedula) — from the Treasurer's Office
- **Barangay clearance** — from your barangay
- The motor's **Certificate of Registration and Official Receipt** from the LTO
- **If the motor is newly bought:** the official receipt or sales invoice
- **If the motor was repossessed or bought from a previous owner:** the deed of
  sale
- **If the motor you bought already has an MTOP from another municipality:** a
  dropping order from that municipality

### Steps

| You                                             | The office                     | Time      |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------- |
| 1. Ask at Window 2, 3 or 7 for the checklist    | Tells you what to secure first | 2 minutes |
| 2. Hand in the requirements                     | Reviews and validates them     | 5 minutes |
| 3. Pay the order of payment at Window 2, 3 or 7 | Issues an official receipt     | 5 minutes |

That is **12 minutes** at the counter, by the charter's own count.

### Fees

The charter does not list them. It says only that the fees "are subject to
Local Revenue Code", which has not been retrieved. It also does not say whether
a renewal needs different documents or fees from a new permit.

## Tricycle: dropping order

If you stop using the tricycle for hire, you apply to drop its permit or
franchise.

**What to bring:** your **latest MTOP** and its **official receipt**.

| You                                             | The office                                        | Time      |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Hand in the documents at Window 6            | Checks them and sends you to the collecting clerk | 5 minutes |
| 2. Pay the order of payment at Window 2, 3 or 7 | Issues an official receipt for the dropping fee   | 5 minutes |

That is **10 minutes** in all.

| Fee                        | Amount                                      |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Dropping of unit           | ₱100.00                                     |
| Change of motor            | ₱100.00                                     |
| "Franchise Tunga/Capoocan" | ₱3,515.00, plus ₱90.00 for police clearance |
| "Franchise Barugo"         | ₱2,494.00, plus ₱90.00 for police clearance |

The two franchise lines are reproduced as the charter prints them. It does not
explain what they are for — Tunga, Capoocan and Barugo are other
municipalities — so ask the Treasurer's Office whether they apply to you.

## Pedicab permit

The pedicab permit lets a pedicab operate within Carigara. It is valid for
**one year from the date it is issued**.

### What to bring

- **Community Tax Certificate** (cedula) — from the Treasurer's Office
- **Barangay clearance** — from your barangay
- **CATEMO clearance / roadworthiness inspection** — from the Carigara Traffic
  Enforcement Management Office

### Steps

| You                                                      | The office                                         | Time      |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Hand in the requirements at Window 6                  | Checks them and sends you to the collecting clerk  | 2 minutes |
| 2. Pay the order of payment at Window 2, 3 or 7          | Issues an official receipt                         | 5 minutes |
| 3. Bring a photocopy of the receipt back for the sticker | Attaches the sticker to the pedicab and records it | 7 minutes |

That is **14 minutes** in all.

### Fees

| Fee                      | Amount      |
| ------------------------ | ----------- |
| Mayor's permit           | ₱100.00     |
| Pedicab registration fee | ₱75.00      |
| Occupation fee           | ₱100.00     |
| Certification fee        | ₱50.00      |
| Health clearance         | ₱50.00      |
| Sticker                  | ₱200.00     |
| Pedicab driver's license | ₱30.00      |
| Secretary's fee          | ₱50.00      |
| Authentication fee       | ₱10.00      |
| Documentary stamp        | ₱30.00      |
| **Total**                | **₱695.00** |

These are the amounts the charter states. The rates themselves are set by the
municipal revenue code, which has not been retrieved.
