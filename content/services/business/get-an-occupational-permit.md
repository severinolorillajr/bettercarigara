# Get an occupational permit

Professionals get an **occupational permit** from the Business Permit and
Licensing Office at the Municipal Hall. Bring the application form, your
cedula, a police clearance and a government ID. The occupation fee is **₱100
per head**, and the whole process takes about **50 minutes** at the counters.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

---

## Where

|         |                                                                           |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Office  | Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO) — Business One Stop Shop area |
| Address | Ground floor, Municipal Hall, E. Makabenta St., Barangay Ponong, Carigara |
| Phone   | 0938 670 3396                                                             |
| Email   | carigarabplo@gmail.com                                                    |
| Who     | The charter says "all professionals"                                      |
| How     | Walk-in                                                                   |

## What to bring

- **Unified Application Form** — get it at the BOSS area, fill it out there and
  hand in the original
- **Community Tax Certificate** (cedula), photocopy — from the Municipal
  Treasurer's Office
- **Police clearance**
- **Government-issued photo ID**, photocopy. If someone applies on your behalf
  with a special power of attorney, bring photocopies of both IDs

## Steps

| You                    | The office                                                                 | Time       |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. File the documents  | BPLO receives, reviews and validates them                                  | 20 minutes |
| 2. Get your assessment | The Treasurer's Office computes the fees and charges                       | 15 minutes |
| 3. Pay and claim       | The Treasurer's cashier issues an official receipt; BPLO issues the permit | 15 minutes |

## Fees

| Fee            | Amount           |
| -------------- | ---------------- |
| Occupation fee | ₱100.00 per head |

The charter gives the total as "Regulatory Fees plus Occupational Permit Fee"
but lists no other amounts, so expect more than the ₱100 and ask BPLO for your
assessment. These are the amounts the charter states, not ordinance rates.

**When to renew.** The charter's description of this service says mayor's
permits "shall be renewed annually, on or before the 20th of January". It does
not say directly whether that deadline applies to occupational permits.
