# Get a mayor's clearance or a permit to hold an activity

A **mayor's clearance** — for employment and other purposes — costs **₱90**
and takes about **10 minutes** once you have your barangay clearance. To hold
an activity in Carigara, you need a separate **mayor's permit**: send a letter
of intent, and once it is approved, pay the assessed fee and claim the permit.
Both are handled by the Municipal Administrator's Office. Neither is a
business permit.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Mayor's clearance

The charter describes this as a clearance "for employment and other essential
purposes".

**What to bring:** your **barangay clearance**, from the barangay where you
live.

| You                                     | The office                      | Time      |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Hand in your barangay clearance      | Stamps it as received           | 2 minutes |
| 2. Pay ₱90 at the Treasurer's Office    | Issues an official receipt      | 3 minutes |
| 3. Bring the receipt back to the office | Prepares your mayor's clearance | 5 minutes |

| Fee               | Amount     |
| ----------------- | ---------- |
| Mayor's clearance | **₱90.00** |

The charter lists "Developers/Property Lot Owners/Organization" as the people
who may apply, which does not match its own description of the service. Ask
the office if you are unsure whether you qualify.

## Mayor's permit to hold an activity

This permit allows a private individual or organization to conduct an activity
within Carigara. The charter covers both private and government activities.

**What to bring:** a **letter of intent** that you write yourself.

| You                                                       | The office                   | Time      |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Submit the letter of intent to the Office of the Mayor | Stamps it as received        | 2 minutes |
| 2. Once it is approved, pay at the Treasurer's Office     | Issues an official receipt   | 5 minutes |
| 3. Bring the receipt back to the office                   | Prepares your mayor's permit | 5 minutes |

The fee depends on the Treasurer's Office's assessment; the charter does not
list amounts. It also does not say how long approval of the letter takes, so
**send the letter well ahead of your activity**.

## Special permit to move a body to another municipality

If you need to transport a deceased relative from Carigara to another
municipality **in an LGU-issued vehicle**, you get a **special permit** from
the Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO), not the Administrator.

|         |                                                                           |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Office  | BPLO — Business One Stop Shop area                                        |
| Address | Ground floor, Municipal Hall, E. Makabenta St., Barangay Ponong, Carigara |
| Phone   | 0938 670 3396                                                             |
| Email   | carigarabplo@gmail.com                                                    |

**What to bring** (walk-in):

- **Unified Application Form** — get it and fill it out at the BOSS area
- A photocopy of a document from the Municipal Treasurer's Office — the
  charter leaves its name blank
- A **government-issued photo ID**, photocopy

| You                    | The office                                                                 | Time       |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. File the documents  | BPLO receives, reviews and validates them                                  | 20 minutes |
| 2. Get your assessment | The Treasurer's Office computes the fees and charges                       | 15 minutes |
| 3. Pay and claim       | The Treasurer's cashier issues an official receipt; BPLO issues the permit | 15 minutes |

That is about **50 minutes** in all. The charter lists no fees for this
permit.

To register the death and get a burial permit, see
[Register a death and arrange a burial](/services/civil-registry/register-a-death-and-arrange-a-burial).
