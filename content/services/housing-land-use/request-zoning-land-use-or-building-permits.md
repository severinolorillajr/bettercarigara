# Get a zoning clearance or land use certification

The Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO) issues ten kinds of zoning
clearance, each a prerequisite for something else: a building or fencing
permit, a land title, an ECC, a land survey, reclassification of farmland, a
subdivision, or a special use. Most take **about an hour to three hours** of
office time plus a **site inspection**, and the fixed-fee ones cost **₱240.00
to ₱590.00**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

"varies, depend[s] on the distance of the site".

## How it works

Every service except excavation follows the same seven steps:

| You                                         | The office                                                                 | Time                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Ask for the checklist                    | Interviews you about the project, its location and the land classification | 5 minutes                                                                                       |
| 2. Hand in your documents                   | Checks they are complete and valid                                         | 15–20 minutes                                                                                   |
| 3. Go with the MPDO inspector to the site   | Inspects the site and writes a report with photos                          | Depends on the distance                                                                         |
| 4. Wait for the result and order of payment | Checks the zoning classification under the Carigara Zoning Ordinance       | 20–30 minutes; **1 hour 50 minutes to 2 hours** when building or fencing plans are also checked |
| 5. Pay at the Treasurer's Office            | Signs the order of payment                                                 | 10 minutes                                                                                      |
| 6. Bring the receipt back to the MPDO       | Prints the certification for the coordinator's signature                   | 5–12 minutes                                                                                    |
| 7. Sign the logbook                         | Releases your copies                                                       | 3–5 minutes                                                                                     |

## What to bring

Almost every service asks for:

- Current year's **tax declaration** (1 original, 1 photocopy) — Municipal
  Assessor's Office
- Current year's **tax clearance** (1 original, 1 photocopy) — Municipal
  Treasurer's Office
- **Technical description** of the lot (or approved subdivision plan or sketch
  plan) — DENR, Tacloban City
- **Barangay clearance** stating its specific purpose — your barangay
- **Proof of lot ownership** — certificate of title, extrajudicial settlement,
  deed of sale or award notice
- One **long expanded envelope**
- For a representative: a notarised authorisation letter or SPA (1 original,
  1 photocopy), and photocopies of the owner's and the representative's
  government IDs (BIR, UMID, postal, voter's, national ID or passport)

What each service adds:

| Service               | Also bring                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Building              | Notarised locational clearance form; building plan, bill of materials and technical specifications (signed and sealed); professionals' PTR and PRC licence. Details on [Get a building permit](/services/housing-land-use/get-a-building-permit) |
| Fencing               | Signed and sealed fencing plan; professionals' PTR and PRC licence; DPWH clearance if along a national road                                                                                                                                      |
| Land titling          | Two certified true copies each of the technical description or subdivision plan, and of the proof of ownership                                                                                                                                   |
| Land reclassification | **Barangay council resolution, minutes and attendance from a public hearing**. No barangay clearance is listed                                                                                                                                   |
| ECC                   | Nothing beyond the common list                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Land survey           | Nothing beyond the common list                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

## Special use permit

Some uses are only allowed by a **Municipal Council resolution**: gasoline and
auto-LPG stations, ice plants, waste disposal facilities, radio and TV
transmitters, telecommunication towers (greenfield), heliports and helipads,
slaughterhouses, cockpits and race tracks, transport terminals and garages,
cemeteries, funeral parlours, memorial chapels and mortuaries, crematoriums and
columbariums.

The charter classifies this as **highly technical**. On top of the building
documents (two sets of plans, two certified true copies of the technical
description and ownership proof), it requires:

- **Barangay council resolution and minutes** (1 original, 1 photocopy)
- An **ECC** from DENR-EMB (1 original, 1 photocopy)
- A notarised **certification of no objection from your neighbours** (1
  original, 1 photocopy)

and, if they apply, structural design and analysis (two storeys and up), DPWH
clearance (national road) and a DAR certification of approved land conversion.

## Subdivisions with housing

Developers of a subdivision with a housing component get a **development
permit with preliminary approval and locational clearance** under P.D. 957 and
B.P. 220. It is a prerequisite for the certificate of registration and licence
to sell. The checklist is long: topographic map, site development plan, road,
drainage, sewer, water and grading plans (two copies each, signed and sealed),
project description, an ECC, an application for a deep-well permit or a certification from the local waterworks, a power
supply application, a zoning certification from the HLURB regional office, a
DAR exemption order or conversion clearance, a traffic impact assessment for
projects of **30 hectares and up**, and for B.P. 220 housing, the housing plans
and a sworn statement on the soundness of the design. Ask the MPDO for the full
checklist.

## Excavation in a road right of way

For contractors and business owners such as **telecommunication and internet
providers** digging in a road right of way. Bring a barangay certification, a
signed and sealed foundation plan and vicinity map, and the professionals'
licences; if they apply, a DPWH clearance, bill of quantities, a letter from
DICT, a DILG memorandum and the current tax clearance. There is no site
inspection step; instead the MPDO schedules a **meeting with you, the affected
barangays and the Sangguniang Bayan Committee on Public Works and
Infrastructure** (1 hour 30 minutes).
