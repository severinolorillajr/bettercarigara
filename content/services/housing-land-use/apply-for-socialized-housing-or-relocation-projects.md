# Get a certificate as a housing program beneficiary

If you are an official beneficiary of **Ecoville 1 and 2, Vergabriel Heights
or the NHA Carigara Housing Projects**, the Municipal Administrator's Office
issues a certificate you need to apply for **water and electric connections**.
It is **free** and takes about **10 minutes**, after a stop at the NHA office.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

**The charter does not describe how to apply for socialized housing or
relocation itself** — who qualifies, where to apply, or how beneficiaries are
chosen. This page covers only the one housing service the charter lists.

---

## Where

|            |                                                                                       |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Office     | Municipal Administrator's Office                                                      |
| Address    | 2nd floor, Municipal Hall, E. Makabenta St., Barangay Ponong, Carigara                |
| Phone      | (053) 888 2903                                                                        |
| Email      | carigaramunicipality@gmail.com                                                        |
| Head       | Atty. Leo L. Pruel, Municipal Administrator                                           |
| First stop | National Housing Authority (NHA), Carigara office — address not listed in the charter |

**Who can get it:** beneficiaries of Ecoville 1 and 2, Vergabriel Heights and
the Carigara Housing Projects (CHP) of the NHA.

## What to bring

- A **barangay certification** from the barangay chairman's office where you
  live
- A **certificate from the NHA** Carigara office (step 1 below)

## Steps

| You                                                                                                                             | The office                                                                                                             | Time      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Go to the NHA Carigara office with your barangay certification and ask for a certification that you are a listed beneficiary | NHA checks your status in its records                                                                                  | 5 minutes |
| 2. Take both documents to the Municipal Administrator's Office                                                                  | Checks them and issues the Certification as Beneficiary of Pabahay, signed by the Mayor or the Municipal Administrator | 5 minutes |

**Fee:** none.
