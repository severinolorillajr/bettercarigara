# Get a building permit

To build, renovate or repair a house in Carigara you go through four steps in
order: a **locational clearance and zoning certification** from the planning
office, the **building permit** itself (about **7 days**), an **electrical
permit** if it applies to you, and a **certificate of occupancy** when the work
is finished (another **7 days**). A permit lapses if work does not start
**within one year**, or stops for **120 days**. A fence needs its own permit.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

For a house built of ordinary materials, the electrical permit is one of the
"ancillary permits" filed with the building permit in step 2, not a separate
trip.

## Step 1 — Locational clearance and zoning certification

The MPDO checks that what you plan to build is allowed on your lot under the
Carigara Zoning Ordinance and the Local Building Code. The building permit
application needs **3 copies each** of the locational clearance and the zoning
clearance.

### What to bring

- Locational Clearance Form, **notarised** (from the MPDO)
- Current year's **tax declaration** — Municipal Assessor's Office
- Current year's **tax clearance** — Municipal Treasurer's Office
- **Technical description** of the lot, certified true copy — DENR, Tacloban
  City
- **Barangay clearance** stating its specific purpose — your barangay
- **Building plan**, bill of materials and technical specifications, one set
  each, signed and sealed by your licensed engineer or architect
- **Proof of lot ownership**, one certified true copy: title, extrajudicial
  settlement or deed of sale. If you do not own the lot: an affidavit of
  consent to construct with the consenting person's government ID, a
  memorandum of agreement, an award notice or a lease contract
- Current PTR and PRC licence of the professionals who signed the plans
- One **long expanded envelope**

Only if they apply: structural design and analysis (**two storeys and up**);
DPWH clearance (**along a national road**); Sangguniang Bayan or barangay
council resolution and minutes, and a DAR certification of approved land
conversion (**non-conforming use or converted land**); ECC/CNC from DENR-EMB.
For a representative: a notarised authorisation letter or SPA, and photocopies
of the owner's and the representative's government IDs.

### Steps

| You                                   | The office                                                                 | Time                                  |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| 1. Ask for the checklist and form     | Interviews you about the project, its location and the land classification | 5 minutes                             |
| 2. Hand in your documents             | Checks they are complete and valid                                         | 15–20 minutes                         |
| 3. Go with the inspector to the site  | Inspects the site and writes a report with photos                          | Depends on the distance               |
| 4. Wait for the result                | Checks the zoning and the plans, then recommends approval or disapproval   | 1 hour 30 minutes, then 20–30 minutes |
| 5. Pay at the Treasurer's Office      | Signs the order of payment                                                 | 10 minutes                            |
| 6. Bring the receipt back to the MPDO | Prints the clearance for the planning coordinator's signature              | 7–12 minutes                          |
| 7. Sign the logbook                   | Releases your copies                                                       | 3 minutes                             |

**Fee:** the charter does not give an amount. It says to "refer to the HLURB
2013 Revised Schedule of Fees".

Some uses need a **special use permit** approved by the Municipal Council — for
example a gasoline station, transport terminal or funeral parlour. Those and
other zoning certifications are on
[Get a zoning clearance or land use certification](/services/housing-land-use/request-zoning-land-use-or-building-permits).

## Step 2 — The building permit

Required before you build, alter, repair, renovate, convert, move or demolish
any building. The application form is **NBC Form B-01**.

### What to bring

| Document                                                                                           | Copies                               | Where to get it                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Barangay clearance                                                                                 | 1 original, 2 photocopies            | Your barangay                                     |
| Latest tax declaration                                                                             | 1 certified true copy, 2 photocopies | Municipal Assessor's Office                       |
| Proof of ownership: OCT or TCT                                                                     | 1 certified true copy, 2 photocopies | Land Registration Authority                       |
| Latest real property tax receipt and tax clearance                                                 | 1 certified true copy, 2 photocopies | Municipal Treasurer's Office                      |
| Locational clearance                                                                               | 3                                    | MPDO (step 1)                                     |
| Zoning clearance                                                                                   | 3                                    | MPDO (step 1)                                     |
| Unified application form, notarised, with ancillary permits signed and sealed by the professionals | 5 each                               | Municipal Engineering Office / your professionals |
| Bill of materials / cost estimate, signed and sealed                                               | 3 sets                               | Your civil engineer or architect                  |
| Technical specifications, signed and sealed                                                        | 3 sets                               | Your civil engineer or architect                  |
| Complete building plans, signed and sealed                                                         | 3 sets                               | Your architect and engineers                      |
| Sketch plan of the land with technical description, approved by DENR                               | 1 certified true copy, 2 photocopies | Geodetic engineer / DENR Tacloban                 |
| PRC IDs and current PTR of the design professionals                                                | 1 original, 2 photocopies            | Your professionals                                |

**If you are not the registered lot owner,** add one of: deed of absolute sale,
notarised affidavit of consent, notarised contract of lease, or extrajudicial
settlement of estate — 1 certified true copy and 2 photocopies.

**Only if they apply:** structural design and analysis for **two storeys and
up** (2 sets); DPWH clearance if you are **along a national road** (DPWH 2nd
Leyte Engineering District, MacArthur, Leyte); ECC/CNC from DENR-EMB for
commercial use. A representative needs a notarised SPA and photocopies of both
people's government IDs.

The ancillary permits are architectural, civil/structural, electrical,
mechanical, sanitary, plumbing and electronics. The charter says the building
permit **is void without them**.

### Steps

| You                                                                                               | The office                                                                                                                                                                                                | Time       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Get the list of requirements (also in your iBPLS account)                                      | Explains the documents and the online process                                                                                                                                                             | 5 minutes  |
| 2. Apply online, or walk in to be helped with it. Bringing building plans in person is encouraged | Reviews the plans and sends the application to the Assessor, MPDO, Bureau of Fire Protection and Treasurer for approval. BFP issues a **fire safety evaluation clearance**. The system computes your fees | **7 days** |
| 3. Pay at the Treasurer's Office                                                                  | Issues an official receipt                                                                                                                                                                                | 15 minutes |
| 4. Receive the permit                                                                             | Approves and issues the building permit with its ancillary and accessory permits                                                                                                                          | 15 minutes |

You can follow the status of the application in your iBPLS account.

**Fee:** depends on the type of building, its floor area and its accessories,
under the schedule of fees of the **National Building Code (P.D. 1096)** and
its rules. The charter does not give amounts.

## Step 3 — Electrical permit for a house of indigenous materials

For a house or shed built of **indigenous materials**, the electrical permit
for the power connection is processed here as its own service. The charter
names LEYECO III for the forms.

### What to bring

- Barangay clearance, latest tax declaration and latest tax receipt or tax
  clearance — **1 photocopy each**
- Electrical permit forms, signed and sealed by a professional electrical
  engineer — 3 copies
- Sketch plan and electrical layout, signed and sealed by the electrical
  engineer and an electrician accredited by LEYECO III — 3 copies
- A **3R photo** showing the whole house — 3 copies
- If you do not own the lot: a deed of absolute sale, notarised affidavit of
  consent from the lot owner, notarised lease contract, or extrajudicial
  settlement — 1 certified true copy
- A notarised **affidavit of undertaking** — 1 original

### Steps

| You                                     | The office                                                | Time       |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Ask for the checklist                | Gives it to you                                           | 5 minutes  |
| 2. Hand in the form, plan and documents | Inspects the house, reviews the plan and computes the fee | 10 minutes |
| 3. Pay at the Treasurer's Office        | Issues an official receipt                                | 15 minutes |
| 4. Return to the Engineering Office     | Logs the application and gives it a permit number         | 15 minutes |
| 5. Go to the Bureau of Fire Protection  | Issues a fire safety evaluation clearance                 | 1 hour     |
| 6. Return to the Engineering Office     | Approves and issues the electrical permit                 | 5 minutes  |

**Fee:** at least **₱280.00**, under the P.D. 1096 schedule of fees.

## Step 4 — Certificate of occupancy

You need this before the building is used. A **partial** certificate can be
issued for finished, independent parts of a building, and a new one is needed
if you change how the building is used.

### What to bring

- Unified application form for a certificate of occupancy, signed and sealed
  by the full-time inspector or supervisor of construction — 3 copies
- Notarised **certificate of completion** — 3 copies
- Certificate of completion for the **electrical works**, signed and sealed by
  an electrical engineer or master electrician — 3 copies
- **As-built plans** and specifications, signed and sealed
- The **daily construction logbook**
- PRC IDs and current PTR of the professionals — 1 original, 2 photocopies

### Steps

| You                                               | The office                                                                                                                   | Time          |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| 1. Get the checklist (also in your iBPLS account) | Gives it to you                                                                                                              | 5 minutes     |
| 2. Apply online or walk in                        | A building inspector checks the building against the approved plans; BFP reviews and inspects; the system computes your fees | **7 days**    |
| 3. Pay at the Treasurer's Office                  | Issues an official receipt                                                                                                   | 15 minutes    |
| 4. Receive the certificate                        | Issues it with the compliance sheet and inspection report                                                                    | 10–15 minutes |

**Fee:** depends on the type of building and its floor area, under the P.D.
1096 schedule of fees. The charter does not give amounts.

## Fencing permit

A fence needs its own permit from the Engineering Office, and that permit needs
a **zoning certification from the MPDO** first.

### First: zoning certification for fencing (MPDO)

Bring the current tax declaration and tax clearance, a certified true copy of
the technical description (DENR Tacloban City), a barangay clearance stating
its purpose, a signed and sealed fencing plan, proof of lot ownership (or one
of the alternatives listed in step 1), the professionals' current PTR and PRC
licence, a DPWH clearance if you are along a national road, and a long expanded
envelope.

The steps are the same as for the locational clearance in step 1 — interview,
document check, site inspection, evaluation, payment, release — about **2½–3
hours** plus the site visit.

| -------------- | ----------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Zoning fee     | ₱150.00     | ₱350.00                 | ₱150.00                |
| Certification  | ₱50.00      | ₱50.00                  | ₱50.00                 |
| Doc. stamp     | ₱30.00      | ₱30.00                  | ₱30.00                 |
| Authentication | ₱10.00      | ₱10.00                  | ₱10.00                 |
| **Total**      | **₱240.00** | **₱440.00**             | **₱240.00**            |

### Then: the fencing permit (Municipal Engineering)

| Document                                                                          | Copies                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Barangay clearance                                                                | 1 original, 1 photocopy            |
| Latest tax declaration                                                            | 1 certified true copy, 1 photocopy |
| Latest real property tax receipt and tax clearance                                | 1 certified true copy, 1 photocopy |
| Application form for fencing permit, notarised                                    | 3                                  |
| Zoning certification from the MPDO                                                | 2                                  |
| Approved survey plan, certified true copy                                         | 2                                  |
| Proof of ownership: OCT or TCT                                                    | 1 certified true copy, 1 photocopy |
| Fencing plans with specifications, signed and sealed                              | 2 sets                             |
| Bill of materials / cost estimate and technical specifications, signed and sealed | 2 sets each                        |
| Sketch plan of the land with technical description, approved by DENR              | 1 certified true copy, 1 photocopy |
| PTR and PRC ID of the professionals                                               | 1 original, 1 photocopy            |
| DPWH clearance — only along a national road                                       | 1 original, 1 photocopy            |

The same alternatives apply if you are not the lot owner, or are sending a
representative, as for the building permit — 1 certified true copy and 1
photocopy each.

| You                               | The office                          | Time          |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------- |
| 1. Ask for the checklist          | Gives it to you                     | 5 minutes     |
| 2. Hand in the form and documents | Evaluates them and computes the fee | **1 day**     |
| 3. Pay at the Treasurer's Office  | Issues an official receipt          | 10–15 minutes |
| 4. Receive the fencing permit     | Issues it                           | 1–5 minutes   |

The charter gives the total as "1 hour and 25 minutes", but its own step 2
takes one day.
