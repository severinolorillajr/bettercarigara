# Get a tax clearance

A real property tax clearance shows that a property's tax is **fully paid**.
You need one to sell a property and to process its transfer at the Bureau of
Internal Revenue and the Registry of Deeds. The Municipal Treasurer's Office
issues it in about **12 minutes** for **₱90.00** per clearance.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

---

## Where

|         |                                                                           |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Office  | Municipal Treasurer's Office — Real Property Tax (RPT) Section            |
| Address | Ground floor, Municipal Hall, E. Makabenta St., Barangay Ponong, Carigara |
| Phone   | (053) 888 2856                                                            |
| Email   | lgu.mtocarigara@gmail.com                                                 |
| Windows | Window 2, 3 or 7                                                          |

**Who can get one:** individuals and corporations who own real property in
Carigara and whose tax is **fully paid**. If you still owe tax, pay it first —
see [Pay real property tax](/services/taxes-property/pay-real-property-tax).

## What to bring

- The official receipt for the **current year** showing full payment — the
  owner's copy of your real property tax receipt

## Steps

| You                                                        | The office                                                                                    | Time      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. At Window 2, 3 or 7, show your latest official receipts | Checks the property against your receipts. If it is fully paid, issues a Tax Order of Payment | 3 minutes |
| 2. Pay ₱90.00 at Window 2, 3 or 7                          | Issues an official receipt                                                                    | 2 minutes |
| 3. Take that receipt to the RPT Section                    | Prepares the clearance; the revenue collection officer and the Municipal Treasurer sign it    | 5 minutes |
| 4. Sign the record book and receive your tax clearance     | Releases the clearance                                                                        | 2 minutes |

| Fee           | Amount     |
| ------------- | ---------- |
| Tax clearance | ₱90.00     |
| **Total**     | **₱90.00** |
