# Get or update a tax declaration

The tax declaration is the official record of a piece of land, a building or
machinery, and the basis for its real property tax. It comes from the
**Municipal Assessor's Office**, not the Treasurer. A certified copy takes
about **an hour** and costs **₱90.00**. The Assessor charges nothing to
transfer, revise or appraise a property (a change of owner needs the transfer
tax paid at the Treasurer's), but the new tax declaration must be approved by
the Provincial Assessor in Palo — allow **about a week**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Get a certified copy or certification

For a print-out or certified true copy of your tax declaration or other
assessment records. Your real property tax must be paid up first.

### What to bring

- The request form — from the Assessor's Office
- Your latest real property tax official receipt
- If you are not the owner: an authorisation letter, or documents showing
  inheritance or heirship

### Steps

| You                                                  | The office                                                      | Time       |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Ask the Assessor's Office for a verification slip | Issues it and gives you information about the property          | 15 minutes |
| 2. Take the slip to the Treasurer's Office           | Checks that the tax is paid. If it is, the request can go ahead | 15 minutes |
| 3. Pay ₱90.00 at the Treasurer's payment window      | Issues an official receipt                                      | 15 minutes |
| 4. Sign the logbook and receive your copy            | Releases the certified copy or certification                    | 15 minutes |

| Fee                | Amount     |
| ------------------ | ---------- |
| Certification fee  | ₱50.00     |
| Documentary stamp  | ₱30.00     |
| Authentication fee | ₱10.00     |
| **Total**          | **₱90.00** |

## Transfer, revise or get a new tax declaration

- **Transfer** — when a property changes owners. It takes effect once you
  present the registered documents.
- **Revision** — to correct an item, or when part of a lot is subdivided under
  an approved plan.
- **New tax declaration for a titled property** — a notice of your application
  is posted for **15 days**.

### What to bring

Certified true copies, **two of each**:

| Document                                                                                                                            | Where to get it                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Proof of ownership: the registered deed of sale or other deed of conveyance, and the BIR Certificate Authorizing Registration (CAR) | Registry of Deeds / Bureau of Internal Revenue |
| Approved subdivision plan — if only part of a lot is involved                                                                       | Registry of Deeds / Bureau of Internal Revenue |
| Certified electronic copy of the title — titled properties                                                                          | Registry of Deeds / Bureau of Internal Revenue |
| Latest real property tax receipt, or a tax clearance                                                                                | Municipal Treasurer's Office                   |
| Transfer tax receipt — for a change of owner                                                                                        | Municipal Treasurer's Office                   |

The **transfer tax** is **3/8 of 1%** of the price or the market value,
whichever is higher. On ₱1,000,000, that is **₱3,750.00**. See also
[Get a tax clearance](/services/taxes-property/get-a-tax-clearance).

### Steps

| You                                                                 | The office                                                                                                                  | Time              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| 1. First time? Ask for the application form and checklist           | Gives you both and briefs you on the requirements                                                                           | 15 minutes        |
| 2. Hand over your documents                                         | Reviews them                                                                                                                | 15 minutes        |
| 3. Submit them for approval by the Provincial Assessor, Palo, Leyte | Prepares the Field Appraisal and Assessment Sheet (FAAS) and the tax declaration, and sends them to the Provincial Assessor | **Within a week** |
| 4. Receive the approved tax declaration                             | Releases the owner's copy with the **Notice of Assessment**                                                                 | 15 minutes        |

The charter's total is **45 minutes, then about a week**. For titled
properties, the 15-day posting suggests it may take longer; the charter does
not reconcile the two.

## New building, new machinery or an improvement

Built a house, installed machinery, or added an improvement such as a fence?
The Assessor's Office inspects it, appraises it and issues an updated tax
declaration.

### What to bring

- The notice of inspection — from the Assessor's Office
- If you are not the owner: a deed of sale or affidavit of consent — from a
  notary public or lawyer

### Steps

| You                                                                | The office                                                                     | Time                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| 1. Get the application form and checklist, and attend the briefing | Issues a notice of inspection                                                  | 15 minutes                           |
| 2. Hand in the completed form and requirements                     | Reviews them, assigns an inspection team and gives you the inspection schedule | 17 minutes                           |
| 3. Guide the team around the property                              | Inspects and appraises the property                                            | Depends on distance, plus 30 minutes |
| 4. Wait for approval                                               | Prepares the FAAS and tax declaration for the Provincial Assessor, Palo        | **Within a week**                    |
| 5. Receive the approved tax declaration                            | Releases the owner's copy with the Notice of Assessment                        | 15 minutes                           |

No fees are listed. The charter's total is **1 hour 17 minutes, then about a
week**, plus travel time for the inspection.
