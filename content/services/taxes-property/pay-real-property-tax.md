# Pay real property tax

Real property tax on land, buildings and machinery is due **every year** and
is paid at the Municipal Treasurer's Office in about **12 minutes**. The
charter puts it at **2% of the assessed value**. Pay in advance, by the **last
working day of the year**, and you get a **20% discount**; pay late and a
**2% penalty per month** is added, with no discount.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

Your property's value for tax purposes is set by the **Municipal Assessor's
Office**; the Treasurer collects the tax. See
[Get or update a tax declaration](/services/taxes-property/get-or-update-a-tax-declaration).

## When to pay

You can pay **annually, semi-annually or quarterly**.

| If you pay…                                     | You pay                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| In advance, by the last working day of the year | 2% of the assessed value, less a **20% discount**                     |
| On time                                         | 2% of the assessed value, less a 10% discount "for the first quarter" |
| Late (delinquent)                               | 2% of the assessed value, plus **2% penalty per month**. No discount  |

For example, on a property assessed at ₱10,000, 2% is **₱200.00** for the
year; paid in advance with the 20% discount, it is **₱160.00**.

The charter does not say whether the 10% discount applies only to payments
made in the first quarter, or what the 2% monthly penalty is computed on.

## What to bring

- Your official receipt from previous years, or the latest property tax
  assessment — the owner's copy of the tax receipt
- A copy of the latest tax declaration of the property — from the Municipal
  Assessor's Office
- A verification slip — from the Municipal Assessor's Office

## Steps

| You                                                                                         | The office                                                                                | Time      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Go to Window 6. If you have no verification slip, you are sent to the Assessor for one   | Refers you to the Municipal Assessor                                                      | 2 minutes |
| 2. At Window 6, hand in the tax declaration, the verification slip and last year's receipts | Checks the owner, tax declaration number, area and location, and issues your **tax bill** | 5 minutes |
| 3. Pay the bill at Window 2, 3 or 7, in cash or by check                                    | Issues an official receipt                                                                | 5 minutes |

Keep the official receipt. You need it for a
[tax clearance](/services/taxes-property/get-a-tax-clearance), for certified
copies of your tax declaration, and to transfer the property.

## If you are behind: demand letter and notice of delinquency

If your tax is unpaid, the Treasurer's Office can issue a **demand letter** and
a **notice of delinquency**. The charter describes a demand letter as a formal
notice of a specific delinquency that is to be paid. Both are recorded in the
office's registry book.

You can also ask for them yourself, to find out exactly what you owe. It takes
about **13 minutes** and is **free**.

| You                                                    | The office                                                  | Time      |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Get a verification slip from the Assessor's Office  | Refers you to the Assessor if you do not have one           | 1 minute  |
| 2. Hand in the verification slip at Window 6           | Computes the tax due on your current and delinquent account | 5 minutes |
| 3. Wait for the result                                 | Tells you what is delinquent and prepares the documents     | 5 minutes |
| 4. Receive the demand letter and notice of delinquency | Issues them and records them in the registry book           | 2 minutes |

**What this means for you:** the documents state what you owe for the current
year and for past years. Penalties keep adding up at 2% per month until you
pay.

**Paying in instalments.** The charter describes a **Compromise Agreement
Scheme**: a written agreement, signed by you and the Municipal Treasurer, to
settle the delinquent tax in instalments within an agreed period.
