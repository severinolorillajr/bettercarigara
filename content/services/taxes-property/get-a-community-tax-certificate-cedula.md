# Get a community tax certificate (cedula)

The community tax certificate — the cedula — is issued at the Municipal
Treasurer's Office in about **10 minutes**. For an individual the basic tax is
**₱5.00**, plus ₱1.00 for every ₱1,000 of income, with the additional tax
capped at **₱5,000**. For a corporation the basic tax is **₱500.00**, plus
₱2.00 for every ₱5,000 of gross receipts, capped at **₱10,000**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Who has to pay

The charter says the community tax is owed by every individual **18 or over**
who:

- has been employed on a wage or salary for at least **30 consecutive working
  days** in a calendar year, or
- is engaged in a business or occupation, or
- owns real property with a total assessed value of **₱1,000 or more**, or
- is required by law to file an income tax return.

Every corporation doing business in the Philippines, domestic or resident
foreign, also pays it.

## What to bring

**Individuals**

- A valid ID
- Your old community tax certificate
- A client information slip — available at the Treasurer's Office

**If you are paying for a business, or for a corporation**

- A valid ID of the owner or the authorised representative
- The old community tax certificate — the charter lists this for businesses,
  but not in its corporation checklist
- The annual gross receipts, certified by the business's accountant

## Steps

| You                                                                            | The office                                    | Time      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. At your window, hand over your ID or the filled-out client information slip | Writes down your details and computes the tax | 5 minutes |
| 2. Pay the amount computed                                                     | Issues the community tax certificate          | 5 minutes |

## How much

**Individuals**

| Tax                                                                              | Amount                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Basic community tax                                                              | ₱5.00                  |
| Additional — gross receipts or earnings from business or a profession, last year | ₱1.00 for every ₱1,000 |
| Additional — income from real property                                           | ₱1.00 for every ₱1,000 |
| Ceiling on the additional tax                                                    | ₱5,000.00              |

**Corporations**

| Tax                                                                                 | Amount                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Basic community tax                                                                 | ₱500.00                |
| Additional — gross receipts or earnings from business in the Philippines, last year | ₱2.00 for every ₱5,000 |
| Additional — assessed value of real property owned in the Philippines               | ₱2.00 for every ₱5,000 |
| Ceiling on the additional tax                                                       | ₱10,000.00             |

For example, by these rates someone who earned ₱120,000 from a profession last
year would pay ₱5.00 + ₱120.00 = **₱125.00**.

These are the amounts the charter states. The rates themselves are set by law
and the municipal revenue code, which has not been retrieved.
