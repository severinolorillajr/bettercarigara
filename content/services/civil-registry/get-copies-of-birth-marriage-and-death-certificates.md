# Get copies of birth, marriage and death certificates

There are two kinds of copy. A **local copy** comes from the Civil Registrar's
own books, costs **₱90** and is ready the same day. A **PSA copy**, printed by the
Philippine Statistics Authority, can be requested through the same office, costs **₱205**, and takes **14 days**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Who can request

Records are released under the **Data Privacy Act**, so not everyone can ask
for one.

- The **owner of the record**, if of age
- The **parents or guardian**, if the owner is a minor
- The **spouse**
- The **nearest relative**, if the owner has died
- Anyone else, with an **authorisation letter** signed by the owner, a
  photocopy of the owner's ID, and a photocopy of their own ID

## Local copy (forms 1A, 2A, 3A)

A certified transcription from the Civil Registrar's books of births (1A),
deaths (2A) or marriages (3A).

| You                                         | The office                                                                             | Time       |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Fill out the request form and hand it in | Checks whether the record exists, issues an order of payment and prepares the document | 75 minutes |
| 2. Pay ₱90 at the Treasurer's Office        | Issues an official receipt                                                             | 20 minutes |
| 3. Return with the receipt                  | You sign the release logbook and receive the document                                  | 35 minutes |

| Fee                | Amount     |
| ------------------ | ---------- |
| Certification fee  | ₱50.00     |
| Documentary stamp  | ₱30.00     |
| Authentication fee | ₱10.00     |
| **Total**          | **₱90.00** |

About **2 hours**, by the charter's count. If there is **no record**, the
office will send you to check with the nearest PSA outlet.

## PSA copy, through the Civil Registrar

The Civil Registrar is a PSA partner under the **Batch Request Entry Query
System (BREQS)**: it takes your request and releases the PSA-printed document
when it arrives.

| You                                              | The office                                                   | Time       |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| 1. Fill out the PSA request form                 | Checks the details and issues an order of payment            | 25 minutes |
| 2. Pay the ₱50 secretary's fee at the Treasurer  | Issues an official receipt                                   | 20 minutes |
| 3. Return with the receipt and pay the PSA fee   | Encodes the request and gives you a **claim stub**           | 45 minutes |
| 4. **After 14 days**, return with the claim stub | Releases the document; you sign the back of the request form | 30 minutes |

| Document                             | PSA fee | With secretary's fee |
| ------------------------------------ | ------- | -------------------- |
| Birth, marriage or death certificate | ₱155.00 | **₱205.00**          |
| CENOMAR (certificate of no marriage) | ₱210.00 | **₱260.00**          |

Bring the **owner's valid ID**, or an authorisation letter with both IDs, and
**one photocopy** of each. The ₱260 CENOMAR total is this page's arithmetic;
the charter gives only the ₱205 total for certificates.
