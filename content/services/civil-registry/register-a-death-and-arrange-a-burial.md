# Register a death and arrange a burial

A death must be registered with the Civil Registrar **within 30 days**. If the
person died **without medical attendance**, the nearest relative must report
it **within 48 hours**. Registration takes about **an hour** and costs **₱60**,
which includes the burial permit.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

---

| Payments | Municipal Treasurer's Office, Windows 2, 3 or 7 — same building           |

**Who can register:** the spouse, a child, the nearest relative who knows of
the death, or anyone with legal charge of the deceased.

## The death certificate

You do not fill this out yourself.

| If the death happened…        | The certificate is prepared by… |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| At Carigara District Hospital | The hospital                    |
| Elsewhere within Carigara     | The Municipal Health Office     |

The Municipal Health Officer examines the deceased, certifies the cause of
death, and directs the registration.

## What to bring

- The filled-out **Certificate of Death** (Municipal Form 103)
- A **government-issued ID** of the informant — the person reporting the death

## Steps

| You                                               | The office                                                   | Time       |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| 1. Hand in the Certificate of Death               | Checks that it is complete and correct                       | 10 minutes |
| 2. Get an order of payment                        | Issues it, assigns a registry number and collects signatures | 20 minutes |
| 3. Pay ₱60 at the Treasurer's Office              | Issues an official receipt                                   | 20 minutes |
| 4. Return to the Civil Registrar with the receipt | Releases your copy of the registered certificate             | 10 minutes |

| Fee             | Amount     |
| --------------- | ---------- |
| Burial permit   | ₱10.00     |
| Secretary's fee | ₱50.00     |
| **Total**       | **₱60.00** |

## Burial at the Municipal Cemetery

For a burial at the **Municipal Cemetery**, there is a separate step at the
**Municipal Administrator's Office** (2nd floor, Municipal Hall).

1. **Bring the death certificate and a request letter.** The office briefs you
   on the cemetery fees and gives you a payment slip.
2. **Pay at the Treasurer's Office.**
3. **Return with the receipt** for an endorsement slip.
4. **Take the endorsement slip to the cemetery caretaker**, who records it and
   shows you the allocated lot.

The cemetery fees the charter lists:

| Fee                           | Amount                                      |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Memorial lot                  | ₱3,000.00                                   |
| Burial                        | ₱50.00                                      |
| Ground maintenance            | ₱200.00                                     |
| Municipal cemetery lot rental | ₱75.00                                      |
| Tomb to be constructed        | ₱100.00                                     |
| **Total**                     | **₱3,425.00** — "good for 10 years renewal" |
| Family lot                    | ₱20,000.00                                  |

These are the amounts the charter states. The charter says the office briefs
you on "the ordinance" that sets them; that ordinance has not been retrieved.
