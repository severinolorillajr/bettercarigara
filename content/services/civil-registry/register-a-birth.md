# Register a birth

A birth must be registered with the Municipal Civil Registrar **within 30 days**.
It is usually done by a parent, but a guardian, the birth attendant, or the
hospital or clinic can file it. It takes about **an hour**, and costs **₱50**,
or **₱200** if the parents are not married.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**. If more than 30 days have passed, see
[Register a birth late](/services/civil-registry/register-a-birth-late).

## What to bring

| Document                                                                             | Where to get it                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Certificate of Live Birth (Municipal Form 102), filled out                           | The Municipal Health Office, hospital or birthing clinic |
| Government-issued ID of the parents                                                  | —                                                        |
| If the parents cannot come: a signed authorisation naming you, and your own valid ID | —                                                        |

**If the parents are not married** and the child will use the father's surname,
also bring a **notarised Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father**. The
father also signs the back of the Certificate of Live Birth.

The charter's checklist for children of married parents also lists an
affidavit of admission of paternity and an affidavit to use the father's
surname. Those normally apply only when the parents are not married — ask the
office whether you need them.

## Steps

| You                                                 | The office                                                   | Time       |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| 1. Hand in the filled-out Certificate of Live Birth | Checks that it is complete and correct                       | 10 minutes |
| 2. Get an order of payment                          | Issues it, assigns a registry number and collects signatures | 20 minutes |
| 3. Pay at the Treasurer's Office                    | Issues an official receipt                                   | 20 minutes |
| 4. Return to the Civil Registrar with the receipt   | Releases your copy of the registered certificate             | 10 minutes |

About **1 hour** in total, by the charter's count.

## Fees

| Parents are… | Fees                                                                                                                                             | Total       |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------- |
| Married      | Secretary's fee ₱50                                                                                                                              | **₱50.00**  |
| Not married  | Secretary's fee ₱50, registration of the affidavit to use the father's surname ₱100, registration of the affidavit of admission of paternity ₱50 | **₱200.00** |

These are the amounts the charter states.
