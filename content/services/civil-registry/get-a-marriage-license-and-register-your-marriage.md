# Get a marriage license and register your marriage

Apply at the Civil Registrar of the place where either of you lives. Each of you
files a separate sworn application. The license is released on the **11th day**
after a public posting, costs **₱477** in total, and is valid anywhere in the
Philippines for **120 days**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## What to bring

Bring **3 photocopies** of each document.

| Document                                                         | Where to get it                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Certificate of Live Birth                                        | PSA, or a local copy from the Civil Registrar |
| Valid ID                                                         | —                                             |
| Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR)                             | Philippine Statistics Authority               |
| Parental **consent** — if you are **18 to 21**                   | Civil Registrar                               |
| Parental **advice** — if you are **21 to 24**                    | Civil Registrar                               |
| Pre-marriage counselling certificate                             | See below                                     |
| Legal capacity to contract marriage — **foreign nationals only** | Your country's embassy or consulate           |

PSA birth certificates and CENOMARs can be requested through the Civil
Registrar too — see
[Get copies of birth, marriage and death certificates](/services/civil-registry/get-copies-of-birth-marriage-and-death-certificates).
They take 14 days, so request them early.

## Steps

| You                                                       | The office                                                                               | Time       |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Hand in your documents                                 | Checks them and **interviews** you both                                                  | 40 minutes |
| 2. Review the details and sign                            | Assigns a registry number; the Civil Registrar signs                                     | 15 minutes |
| 3. Get an order of payment and pay ₱475 at the Treasurer  | Prepares a **notice of marriage** and posts it on the LGU bulletin board for **10 days** | 45 minutes |
| 4. Show the receipt to the Civil Registrar                | Sends you to the POPCOM office to schedule pre-marriage counselling                      | 10 minutes |
| 5. On the **11th day**, return and pay ₱2 for the license | Issues the license                                                                       | 25 minutes |
| 6. Collect the license                                    | Gives you two copies of the application: one for you, one for the solemnizing officer    | 20 minutes |

| Marriage license | ₱2.00       |
| **Total**        | **₱477.00** |

These are the amounts the charter states. The charter classifies the license as
a "highly technical" transaction.

## Pre-marriage counselling

Every couple must attend a **pre-marriage orientation and counselling session**
with an accredited counsellor. It is run by the Municipal Social Welfare and
Development Office with POPCOM.

1. Fill out the application at the MSWDO (ground floor, Municipal Hall).
2. Fill out the Marriage Inventory Expectations questionnaire.
3. Attend the session on the date you are given — **2 to 3 hours**.
4. Collect your certificate.

The charter lists no fee at the MSWDO itself. The ₱75 "PMC fee" above is paid
with the license application.

## Getting married by the Mayor

The Mayor can solemnize marriages. Register in the logbook at the Mayor's
Office, bring your marriage license and its attached documents, pay the
**₱500 solemnization fee** at the Treasurer's Office, and present the receipt.

## After the wedding: registering the marriage

The **certificate of marriage** must reach the Civil Registrar of the place
where the marriage was solemnized within **15 days**, or **30 days** for a
marriage exempt from the license requirement. The certificate is prepared at
the solemnizing officer's office.

Registering it in Carigara takes about **an hour** and costs **₱50**
(secretary's fee): hand it in, get the order of payment, pay at the Treasurer,
and collect your copy.
