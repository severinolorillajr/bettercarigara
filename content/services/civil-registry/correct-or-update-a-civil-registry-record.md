# Correct or update a civil registry record

Many mistakes on a birth, marriage or death certificate can be fixed at the
Civil Registrar **without going to court**. Which process you need depends on
what is wrong — and the cost runs from **₱290** to over **₱5,000**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Which process do you need?

| What is wrong                                                                   | Process                  | Total  | How long            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------ | ------------------- |
| Something was **left blank** when it was registered                             | Supplemental report      | ₱290   | About 2 hours       |
| A child of unmarried parents should **use the father's surname**                | RA 9255                  | ₱570   | About 3 hours       |
| The parents **married after** the child was born                                | Legitimation             | ₱670   | About 3 hours       |
| An obvious **typing or spelling mistake** — a misspelled name or place of birth | Clerical error (RA 9048) | ₱1,852 | About **3½ months** |
| Change of **first name**, of the **day or month** of birth, or of **sex**       | RA 9048 and RA 10172     | ₱5,217 | About **4 months**  |

Bring **3 photocopies** of each document for every process. After each one, you
receive the owner's copy and are told to take the PSA copy to the PSA regional
office (RSSO 8) so PSA's own record is annotated too.

---

## Supplemental report — filling in a blank entry

For entries **omitted** at registration. It is **not** for correcting an entry
that is there but wrong.

**Bring:** the PSA and local copies of the certificate, a notarised affidavit to
supply the missing data, and any document that proves the missing entry — a
baptismal certificate, marriage certificate, the parents' birth certificates,
or a medical record.

| Fee                           | Amount      |
| ----------------------------- | ----------- |
| Filing of supplemental report | ₱50.00      |
| Processing                    | ₱100.00     |
| Certification                 | ₱50.00      |
| Documentary stamp             | ₱30.00      |
| Authentication                | ₱10.00      |
| Secretary's fee               | ₱50.00      |
| **Total**                     | **₱290.00** |

The charter's payment step says ₱240.00, but its itemised fees and its total
both come to ₱290.00.

## RA 9255 — using the father's surname

For children of unmarried parents born on or after **3 August 1988** whose
father acknowledges them.

**Bring:** the PSA and local copies of the birth certificate, a notarised
affidavit of admission of paternity (signed by the father), a notarised
affidavit to use the surname of the father, and the parents' valid IDs.

**Who signs the affidavit to use the surname:**

| Child's age  | Signed by                                           |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 0 to 6       | The mother                                          |
| 7 to 17      | The child, with a sworn attestation from the mother |
| 18 and above | The child, without the mother's attestation         |

Fees: processing ₱100, secretary's fee ₱50, affidavit of paternity ₱50,
affidavit to use the surname ₱100, and three sets of certification ₱50,
documentary stamp ₱30 and authentication ₱10 — **₱570.00** in total.

## Legitimation

When the parents **marry after** the child's birth, and had no legal impediment
to marry when the child was conceived, the child becomes legitimate.

**Bring:** the PSA and local copies of the birth certificate, a notarised
affidavit of admission of paternity, a notarised affidavit of legitimation, and
the parents' valid IDs.

Fees: processing ₱100, secretary's fee ₱50, affidavit of paternity ₱50,
affidavit of legitimation ₱200, and three sets of certification ₱50,
documentary stamp ₱30 and authentication ₱10 — **₱670.00** in total.

## Clerical error (RA 9048)

For mistakes that are harmless and obvious on their face — a misspelled name or
place of birth.

**Who can file:** the owner of the record, or their spouse, children, parents,
siblings, grandparents, guardian, or anyone authorised by law or by the owner.

**Bring:** the birth certificate (PSA and local copies), baptismal certificate,
Form 137-E from your last elementary school, voter's certification, marriage
contract, valid IDs, and your children's birth certificates.

1. Hand in the documents and pay the **₱1,000 filing fee**.
2. The petition is filled out with you; review it carefully and sign.
3. It is **posted for 10 consecutive days**. The Civil Registrar then has
   **5 days** to decide.
4. If granted, it is sent to the **PSA Legal Division** (courier ₱182). The
   charter estimates **3 months** for PSA's decision.
5. Once approved, pay **₱670** for the annotated copies, and collect them with
   a certificate of finality.

| Stage                                                                                                                      | Amount        |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Filing fee                                                                                                                 | ₱1,000.00     |
| Courier to PSA                                                                                                             | ₱182.00       |
| Processing ₱100, certified true copies ₱300 (6), certification ₱150 (3), documentary stamp ₱90 (3), authentication ₱30 (3) | ₱670.00       |
| **Total**                                                                                                                  | **₱1,852.00** |

## First name, birth day or month, or sex (RA 9048 and RA 10172)

**Change of first name** is allowed when the name is ridiculous, tainted with
dishonour, or very hard to write or pronounce; when you have habitually and
publicly used another first name; or when the change avoids confusion. The same
process covers the **day and month** of the date of birth, and **sex**.

For a change of sex, you must **file and appear in person**.

**Bring** everything listed for a clerical error, plus: NBI clearance, police
clearance, a certificate of employment showing no pending administrative or
criminal case (or an affidavit of unemployment), and — later — the affidavit of
publication and newspaper clippings.

The steps are the same as for a clerical error, with one addition: after the
10-day posting, the petition must be **published in a newspaper**, which the
charter estimates at 14 days.

| Stage                       | Amount        |
| --------------------------- | ------------- |
| Filing fee                  | ₱3,000.00     |
| Newspaper publication       | ₱1,365.00     |
| Courier to PSA              | ₱182.00       |
| Annotated copies (as above) | ₱670.00       |
| **Total**                   | **₱5,217.00** |
