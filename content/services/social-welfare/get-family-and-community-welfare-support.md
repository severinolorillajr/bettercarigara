# Get help in a crisis, a certificate of indigency or a case study

The MSWDO gives **cash help** to indigent families facing a medical bill, a
death, a trip home or an empty table (**AICS**), and separate help for
**persons with disability**. It also writes the papers other agencies ask for:
a **certificate of indigency** and a **social case study report**. All are
**free**. The papers take **under an hour** to about **2 hours**; cash help
takes **1 hour to about 3¼ hours** at the office by the charter's step times.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Which one you need

| Your situation                                                                                                                                        | Ask for                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| You need money **now** for a hospital bill, medicine, a procedure, a funeral, food, or fare home                                                      | **AICS** — Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation |
| You are a **person with disability** needing medical help or a mobility or assistive device                                                           | **PWD financial assistance**                             |
| Another agency is helping you with medical, financial, burial or school costs and wants a **case study**                                              | **Social case study report**                             |
| You need **proof you are indigent** — for educational, medical, financial or burial aid, PhilHealth, or a free lawyer at the Public Attorney's Office | **Certificate of indigency**                             |

Start at your **barangay**: almost everything below needs a barangay
certificate of indigency or residency first.

## AICS — cash help in a crisis

AICS is a Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) programme that
the MSWDO runs locally. The charter describes it as help "to sustain the basic
necessities of the families suffering from economic inadequacies".

### What to bring

Every request needs a **valid ID** of the client or of the person being
interviewed, and a **barangay certificate of residency or certificate of
indigency** from your barangay chairman. Then, by type:

| Help for…     | Also bring                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Medical costs | Medical certificate or medical abstract; the hospital bill or statement of account (for a bill); a prescription with date, the doctor's full name, signature and licence number (for medicines or assistive devices); for chemotherapy or dialysis, the doctor's request with estimated cost and treatment protocol; for CT scan, ultrasound, MRI or X-ray, the laboratory's charge slip |
| A burial      | Death certificate and funeral contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Moving a body | Death certificate and transfer permit (Civil Registrar, sanitary inspector or Municipal Health Office)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Transport     | A police blotter or certification if you were robbed, illegally recruited or similar; other supporting papers such as a social worker's referral, medical certificate, death certificate or court order or subpoena                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Food          | Nothing more                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

### Steps

| You                               | The office                                                                                                                                                         | Time                   |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| 1. Tell MSWDO staff what you need | Intake interview; passes you to a worker for assessment and tells you which documents to get                                                                       | 30 minutes to 1 hour   |
| 2. Present the documents          | Reviews them (5 minutes), a social worker assesses and validates your situation (30 minutes to 1 hour), and if you are eligible a voucher is prepared (10 minutes) | 45 minutes to 1¼ hours |
| 3. Claim the assistance           | Releases the cash                                                                                                                                                  | 30 minutes to 1 hour   |

The charter prints no total. Its step times add up to **1 hour 45 minutes to
3 hours 15 minutes**, not counting time spent collecting documents. It does
not say how much assistance is given.

## PWD financial assistance

Run by the Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO) within the MSWDO.

### What to bring

| For medical help (AICS)                                                                                                                                                                                                             | For a mobility or assistive device                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Original, filled-out **barangay indigency** of the claimant, for medical assistance                                                                                                                                                 | **Barangay indigency** in the claimant's name, for assistive device assistance                     |
| Photocopy of the claimant's ID, both sides, with original signature or thumbmark                                                                                                                                                    | Photocopy of the claimant's ID, both sides, signed or thumbmarked                                  |
| Photocopy of the **PWD ID**, both sides, with original signature or thumbmark                                                                                                                                                       | Photocopy of the **PWD ID**, both sides, signed or thumbmarked                                     |
| Original or certified true copy of the medical certificate or clinical abstract, with the doctor's licence number — plus a prescription, a lab request, a chemotherapy or dialysis treatment protocol, or a tentative hospital bill | A **whole-body picture** of the person with disability, any size, where the disability can be seen |

No PWD ID yet? See
[Apply for a senior citizen, solo parent or PWD ID](/services/social-welfare/apply-for-senior-citizen-solo-parent-or-pwd-assistance).

### Steps

| You                                                                       | The office                                                                                                                                                                | Time       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Submit the documents with a filled-out PDAO assistance assessment form | Reviews and interviews (10 minutes), approves (5 minutes); an MSWDO social worker assesses you, prepares a transaction slip and has you sign an intake sheet (30 minutes) | 45 minutes |
| 2. —                                                                      | Prepares the disbursement voucher and sends it to the Budget Office                                                                                                       | 15 minutes |

**Total: 1 hour.** The charter stops at the voucher going to the Budget
Office. It does not say when or how the money reaches you, or how much it is.

## Certificate of indigency

Proof from the municipality that you are indigent, for educational, medical,
financial or burial assistance, and for free legal help from the Public
Attorney's Office (PAO).

### What to bring

| Document                                                                              | Where to get it             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Barangay indigency**, stating that family income does not exceed family consumption | Your barangay hall          |
| Certificate of no property                                                            | Municipal Assessor's Office |
| Affidavit of tax exemption — for PhilHealth and PAO                                   | PAO or BIR                  |
| Income tax document                                                                   | BIR                         |

The charter writes "Income Tax" without saying which document.

### Steps

| You                              | The office                  | Time       |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Come in person and ask for it | Interviews and assesses you | 15 minutes |
| 2. Present the documents         | Reviews them                | 15 minutes |
| 3. Claim the certificate         | Releases it                 | 2 minutes  |

The charter gives the total as **10 minutes**, but its steps add up to **32
minutes**.

## Social case study report

A social worker's written report on your situation. The charter says it is
for residents seeking medical, financial, burial or educational help from
**other agencies**, under the municipality's expanded AICS. The same step also
covers a **parents capability assessment report** and assessment reports for
**minors travelling abroad**.

### What to bring

| Document                                                                              | Where to get it        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Medical certificate, medical abstract or lab request                                  | A doctor               |
| Updated hospital bill, or the latest prescription **with the amount shown**           | The hospital or doctor |
| **Barangay indigency**, stating that family income does not exceed family consumption | Your barangay chairman |
| Valid ID                                                                              | You                    |

### Steps

| You                              | The office                                      | Time         |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| 1. Come in person and ask for it | Intake interview                                | 15 minutes   |
| 2. Present the documents         | Reviews them; a social worker writes the report | 1 to 2 hours |
| 3. Claim the report              | Releases it                                     | 5 minutes    |

The charter gives the total as **2 hours 15 minutes**; its steps add up to
**1 hour 20 minutes to 2 hours 20 minutes**.
