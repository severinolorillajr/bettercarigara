# Join a livelihood, TUPAD or skills training programme

The Public Employment Service Office (PESO) refers Carigara residents to three
national programmes: **DOLE livelihood funding** (DILP) for individuals and
groups, **TUPAD** temporary work for disadvantaged and displaced workers, and
**skills training** with agencies such as TESDA and DICT. All are **free** to
apply for. TUPAD registration takes under **2 hours**; a livelihood proposal
takes about **4 days** to reach DOLE; a training needs **25 applicants**
before PESO proposes it.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

PESO only **refers** you. The money, the work and the training come from the
national agency — DOLE for DILP and TUPAD, and TESDA, DICT or another agency
for training. The charter's steps end when PESO sends your papers on.

## DILP — DOLE Integrated Livelihood Program

For residents who want funding to start or grow a livelihood. The charter
describes its aim as "to uplift the living condition of disadvantaged /
marginalized people of Carigara". It is classed as a "highly technical"
transaction.

### What to bring

| As an individual                                                        | As a group                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Letter of intent stating the livelihood you want                        | The organisation's resolution stating the proposed livelihood            |
| Latest financial statement                                              | Organisation profile, with the list of members and officers              |
| Business permit, if any — from the Business Permit and Licensing Office | Latest financial statement                                               |
|                                                                         | Constitution and by-laws                                                 |
|                                                                         | Sangguniang Bayan accreditation — from the Sangguniang Bayan of Carigara |
|                                                                         | DOLE accreditation — from the Department of Labor and Employment         |
|                                                                         | Business permit, if any — from the Business Permit and Licensing Office  |

### Steps

| You                                     | The office                                                                                                                 | Time                                   |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 1. Submit the requirements              | Receives them, then interviews an individual, or sets a meeting with a group's members                                     | 30 minutes (individual), 1 day (group) |
| 2. Fill in the beneficiary profile form | Verifies and assesses you (1 hour). If you qualify, gets a letter of intent signed by the Mayor (1 day); if not, tells you | 1 day and 1 hour                       |
| —                                       | Writes the final proposal from your request                                                                                | 2 days                                 |
| —                                       | Submits it to DOLE for funding                                                                                             | 1 day                                  |

The charter gives the total as **4 days, 1 hour and 30 minutes**. That matches
an individual applicant; for a group, whose first step takes a day, the same
steps add up to **5 days and 1 hour**. After that, the decision is DOLE's.

## TUPAD — temporary work for disadvantaged and displaced workers

**Tulong Panghanapbuhay para sa Ating Disadvantaged/Displaced Workers** is a DOLE
programme. Through it, PESO arranges temporary employment for disadvantaged
and displaced workers, and for the underemployed and self-employed.

### What to bring

- A **government-issued ID**
- **Senior citizens:** a **fit-to-work certification** from the Rural Health
  Unit

### Steps

| You                                    | The office                                                                                          | Time       |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Fill out the NSRP registration form | Receives it                                                                                         | 20 minutes |
| 2. Submit your requirements            | Receives them, interviews you, and encodes your details in the Public Employment Information System | 20 minutes |
| —                                      | Emails the list of beneficiaries to DOLE for validation                                             | 1 hour     |

**Total: 1 hour and 40 minutes.** DOLE then validates the list; the charter
does not say how long that takes or when work starts.

## Skills training referral

PESO refers qualified applicants to training that the charter says improves
their chances of employment or their productivity at work.

### What to bring

| Document                          | Where to get it                 |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Application form                  | PESO                            |
| Passport-size ID picture          | You                             |
| Birth certificate                 | Philippine Statistics Authority |
| Marriage certificate — if married | Philippine Statistics Authority |

### Steps

| You                                                                  | The office                                                                                                                                                   | Time  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----- |
| 1. Ask for an application form, then submit it with the requirements | Accepts them                                                                                                                                                 | 1 day |
| —                                                                    | Once there are **at least 25 applicants** for a particular training, writes a project proposal and submits it to the agency concerned, such as TESDA or DICT | 1 day |

**Total: 2 days** — counted from the point that 25 people have applied for the
same training. Until then, your application waits.
