# Get a medical or medico-legal certificate

The Municipal Health Office issues **medical certificates** for a job, school
entrance or a medical excuse, and **medico-legal certificates** for legal
purposes. The fee is **₱50** and it takes about **20 minutes** once your test
results are ready.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## What to bring

**For employment**, results from a laboratory for:

- Blood test
- Urinalysis
- Chest X-ray
- Drug test
- Neuro-psychiatric test
- Physical examination, and any other test the office thinks necessary

**For food handlers**, results from a laboratory for:

- Stool exam
- Hepatitis B screening (HBsAg)
- Sputum exam

**For a medico-legal certificate**, the charter lists no documents. The
Municipal Health Officer examines you instead.

**In every case**, the **receipt** for the ₱50 fee.

## Steps

| You                                           | The office                                                                                          | Time       |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Ask at the Municipal Health Office         | Tells you what you need for your kind of certificate                                                | 2 minutes  |
| 2. Present your receipt and your requirements | For employment, checks your laboratory and X-ray results. For medico-legal, sends you to the doctor | 15 minutes |
| 3. Wait for the certificate                   | Prepares and records it                                                                             | 3 minutes  |

| Fee         | Amount     |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| Certificate | ₱50.00     |
| **Total**   | **₱50.00** |

Opening a food business? You also need a sanitary permit — see
[Apply for barangay clearance and mayor's business permits](/services/business/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits).
