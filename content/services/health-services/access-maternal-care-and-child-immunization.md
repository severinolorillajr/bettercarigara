# Get your baby vaccinated, pregnancy check-ups and family planning

Vaccination for babies, pre-natal and post-natal check-ups, and family planning
all start at **your own barangay health station**, and all three are **free**.
A vaccination visit takes about **20 minutes**, a pre-natal visit about **52
minutes**, and a family planning visit about **40 minutes**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Vaccinate your baby

The **Expanded Program on Immunization** is for babies **0 to 11 months old**.
The vaccines the charter lists:

- **BCG** and **Hepatitis B**, both given at birth
- **Oral polio vaccine**
- **Pentavalent vaccine**
- **Measles-containing vaccines** — anti-measles, and measles-mumps-rubella

The programme also gives **tetanus toxoid** to mothers (see pre-natal care
below).

### What to bring

- Your child's **Early Child Care Development (ECCD) card**, if your child has
  had vaccines before. If not, the midwife fills one out for you.

### Steps

| You                                                   | The health station                                                                    | Time       |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Go to your barangay health station to ask about it | The midwife explains the programme and gives you the vaccination schedule             | 2 minutes  |
| 2. Come back on the scheduled date with your child    | Fills out or updates the ECCD card, weighs your baby, gives the vaccine and vitamin A | 15 minutes |
| 3. Wait for the after-vaccination instructions        | Watches for any reaction and gives you the next date                                  | 3 minutes  |

**Keep the ECCD card.** You bring it to every vaccination.

## Pre-natal and post-natal care

For **all pregnant and breastfeeding women**.

### What to bring

- A **valid ID** — PhilHealth, SSS, GSIS, BIR, or another government-issued ID

### Steps

| You                                   | The health station                                                                                                                                                     | Time                   |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| 1. Go to your barangay health station | The midwife records your details and medical history and works out how far along you are and your expected due date                                                    | 10 minutes             |
| 2. Have a pre-natal examination       | Checks for danger signs, gives iron and vitamin A and the tetanus toxoid vaccine, and helps you make a birth plan. Refers you to the Municipal Health Office if needed | 20 minutes             |
| 3. Counselling                        | Covers the 10 danger signs of pregnancy, fertility awareness and family planning, and sets your next visit                                                             | 5 minutes + 15 minutes |
| 4. Ask for your next check-up         | Gives you your visit schedule                                                                                                                                          | 2 minutes              |

You may be referred for routine laboratory tests: a **complete blood count,
blood typing, urinalysis and hepatitis B screening**. A syphilis test (VDRL) or
an ultrasound is added only if needed.

### How often to come back

| When                     | How often                                |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| Until the 7th month      | **Every month**                          |
| During the 8th month     | **Every 2 weeks**                        |
| From then until delivery | **Every week** (normal cases)            |
| After you give birth     | **2 to 3 check-ups** within 4 to 6 weeks |

## Family planning

For **couples and women of reproductive age**. Nothing to bring.

| You                                                                 | The health station                                                                    | Time       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Go to your barangay health station to ask which method suits you | The midwife interviews you and opens a family planning record                         | 2 minutes  |
| 2. Have a physical examination                                      | Refers you to the Municipal Health Officer for an examination; asks about a Pap smear | 20 minutes |
| 3. If you had a bad reaction to a method before                     | The Municipal Health Officer treats it and advises a different method                 | 15 minutes |
| 4. Go back to your midwife                                          | Gives you the method and a **family planning card**, and sets a follow-up visit       | 3 minutes  |
