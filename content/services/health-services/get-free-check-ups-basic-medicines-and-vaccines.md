# Get free check-ups, basic medicines and vaccines

Primary health care in a municipality is delivered through the **Rural Health
Unit (RHU)** and its barangay health stations. Health services were devolved to
local governments by the Local Government Code, so the RHU is run by the
Municipality of Carigara, not by the national Department of Health.

Carigara's own RHU location, schedule and staffing are not published here yet —
see the gap list below.

---

## Register for PhilHealth YAKAP

This is the single most useful thing most residents can do, and it is free.

**YAKAP** — _Yaman ng Kalusugan Program_ — is PhilHealth's primary care benefit
package. It was launched on **25 July 2025**, replacing and expanding the earlier
Konsulta programme. If you have read older guidance referring to "Konsulta", this
is what it became.

Once you register and pick an accredited provider, the package covers:

- **Consultations** with your chosen provider
- **Medicines** — 75 listed drugs, up from 21 under Konsulta
- **Laboratory and screening tests** — 19, up from 13, now including screening
  for breast, lung, liver and colorectal cancer

**How to register:** through the PhilHealth member portal at
[philhealth.gov.ph](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/yakap/), or in person at a
PhilHealth office with a valid ID. Registration means choosing the accredited
facility you will use — in a municipality, that is commonly the RHU.

You must be registered **before** you need care. Registering at the point of
illness is the most common reason people miss out on benefits they were already
entitled to.

## What is not published yet

_None of the following is verified for Carigara._

- Whether Carigara's RHU is an accredited YAKAP provider
- RHU location and opening hours
- Consultation schedule, and which days have a physician on duty
- Immunisation schedule for infants and children
- Barangay health stations and their schedules across the 49 barangays
- Contact number for the Municipal Health Office
- Which medicines are actually in stock, which is a different question from
  which are covered

If you have used the RHU recently, you know more than this page does. See the
repository README — corrections are the most useful contribution this site takes.
