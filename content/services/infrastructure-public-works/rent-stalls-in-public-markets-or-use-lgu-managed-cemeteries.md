# Buy or rent a lot at the Municipal Cemetery

You can buy or rent a grave lot or niche at the **Municipal Cemetery**. Check
with the cemetery caretaker which lots are available, then pay at the Municipal
Treasurer's Office — about **10 minutes** in all. A memorial lot package costs
**₱3,425**, "good for 10 years renewal"; a family lot costs **₱20,000**. The
charter does not cover renting stalls in the public market.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

---

## Where

|         |                                                                           |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lots    | The caretaker at the Carigara Municipal Cemetery                          |
| Payment | Municipal Treasurer's Office, Windows 2, 3 or 7                           |
| Address | Ground floor, Municipal Hall, E. Makabenta St., Barangay Ponong, Carigara |
| Phone   | (053) 888 2856                                                            |
| Email   | lgu.mtocarigara@gmail.com                                                 |

The charter does not give the cemetery's address.

**Arranging a burial?** A burial at the Municipal Cemetery also goes through
the Municipal Administrator's Office, which gives you the payment slip and,
after you pay, an endorsement slip for the caretaker. See
[Register a death and arrange a burial](/services/civil-registry/register-a-death-and-arrange-a-burial).

## Steps

| You                                                             | The office                   | Time      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Ask the cemetery caretaker which lots or niches are for sale | Shows you the available lots | 5 minutes |
| 2. Present your payment slip at Window 2, 3 or 7                | Issues an official receipt   | 5 minutes |

Keep the **official receipt** — the charter lists it as your proof.

## Fees

| Fee                           | Amount                                      |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Memorial lot                  | ₱3,000.00                                   |
| Burial                        | ₱50.00                                      |
| Ground maintenance            | ₱200.00                                     |
| Municipal cemetery lot rental | ₱75.00                                      |
| Tomb to be constructed        | ₱100.00                                     |
| **Total**                     | **₱3,425.00** — "good for 10 years renewal" |
| Family lot                    | ₱20,000.00                                  |

All money from sales and rentals goes to the Municipal Treasurer to pay for
maintaining the grounds and cemetery services. These are the amounts the
charter states; it says prices are set by the municipal revenue code, which has
not been retrieved.

## Market stalls

The charter does **not** describe how to rent a stall in the public market. It
does mention the market clearance that public market vendors need for a
business permit, from the Office of Market and Slaughter — see
[Apply for a barangay clearance and a mayor's business permit](/services/business/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits).
