# Get your dog vaccinated against rabies

The Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) vaccinates dogs against rabies **for
free**. You can walk in at the MAO, or bring your dog to the **yearly
vaccination drive** held in every barangay. Dogs must be **at least 3 months
old**. It takes about **35 minutes**. This is the only animal health service
the charter lists for the MAO.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

**The barangay drive** is held **once a year in each barangay**, at a
designated site, date and time. Barangay officials are expected to let dog
owners know the schedule.

## Who can bring a dog

**Registered dog owners** in Carigara. The charter lists "Registered Dog Owners
in the Barangay" as the requirement, to be secured at the **barangay hall**. It
does not explain how to register; ask your barangay hall.

## What to bring

- **Your dog**, at least 3 months old
- Your details and your dog's details

## Steps

The steps are the same at the MAO and at the barangay drive.

| You                                                  | The office                    | Time       |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Bring the dog and give your details and the dog's | Records them                  | 15 minutes |
| 2. Hold or restrain your dog during the injection    | Gives the anti-rabies vaccine | 15 minutes |
| 3. Fill out the CSM feedback form                    | Collects it                   | 5 minutes  |

Total: about **35 minutes**. No fees.

The charter's own total says "1 hour and 10 minutes", which adds the walk-in
and barangay-drive steps together; each route on its own is 35 minutes.

## Other animals

The charter lists no veterinary service for livestock — no treatment,
vaccination or check-up for pigs, cattle, carabao, goats or poultry. Livestock
owners can insure animals through the MAO; see
[Get help with crop pests, soil testing and insurance](/services/agriculture-fisheries/get-help-with-crop-pests-soil-testing-and-insurance).
For slaughter, see
[Use the municipal slaughterhouse](/services/agriculture-fisheries/use-the-municipal-slaughterhouse).
