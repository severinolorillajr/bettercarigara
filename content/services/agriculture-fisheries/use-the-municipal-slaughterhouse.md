# Use the municipal slaughterhouse

Meat vendors and others can have **cattle, carabao and swine** slaughtered and
inspected at the Carigara Municipal Slaughterhouse. The charter's example fees
come to **₱504.32 per head** for cattle or carabao and **₱201.66 per head** for
swine, and the process takes about **26 minutes**. Butchers renew their
butcher's permit at the same office for **₱940**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Slaughtering cattle, carabao and swine

**Who can use it:** meat vendors and others who need slaughtering.

### What to bring

| Document                                                                   | Where to get it                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Barangay certification                                                     | Barangay hall                                 |
| Proof of buying and trading the animal, witnessed by the Barangay Chairman | Barangay hall                                 |
| Transport permit                                                           | Police station                                |
| Veterinary health certificate                                              | Department of Agriculture / veterinary office |
| **Original** Certificate of Ownership of Large Cattle (COLC)               | Revenue collector                             |
| Certificate of Transfer                                                    | Revenue collector                             |
| Police clearance of the animal from its place of origin                    | Police station                                |
| Entrance slip and official receipt for the slaughter permit                | Revenue collector                             |

The charter gives one list for all animals. It does not say which documents,
such as the COLC, apply only to cattle and carabao.

### Steps

| You                                                                                      | The office                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Time       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| 1. Bring the animal to the transaction area to be logged and weighed                     | The guard records the animal and your details and takes your shipping permit and veterinary health certificate. The animal is weighed, you get an entrance slip, and the revenue clerk issues the ownership papers | 5 minutes  |
| 2. The animal goes to the stockyard; hand in all your requirements                       | The meat inspector examines the animal before slaughter (ante-mortem) and isolates it                                                                                                                              | 5 minutes  |
| 3. The animal goes to a holding pen; present the slaughter permit approved by the police | The meat inspector takes the permit and signals the butcher to slaughter                                                                                                                                           | 2 minutes  |
| 4. Pay the fees                                                                          | The meat inspector checks the carcass (post-mortem). If it passes, the meat and offal are stamped for transport to the public market or a meat shop                                                                | 10 minutes |
| 5. Receive the carcass and certificates                                                  | The delivery van takes the meat to the public market and meat shops. You get a Meat Inspection Certificate and Condemnation Slip                                                                                   | 4 minutes  |

Total: about **26 minutes**.

### Fees, per head

| Fee                              | Cattle / carabao | Swine       |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------- | ----------- |
| Ante-mortem fee                  | ₱5.00            | ₱3.00       |
| Post-mortem fee (see below)      | ₱226.32          | ₱98.46      |
| Livestock commission             | ₱13.00           | ₱5.70       |
| Slaughter fee                    | ₱100.00          | ₱50.00      |
| Stockyard fee                    | ₱10.00           | ₱1.50       |
| Holding pen fee                  | ₱15.00           | ₱8.00       |
| Weights and measures             | ₱25.00           | ₱10.00      |
| Delivery fee                     | ₱50.00           | ₱20.00      |
| Livestock Development Fund       | ₱5.00            | ₱5.00       |
| Bureau of National Treasury fund | ₱5.00            | —           |
| Police inspection fee            | ₱50.00           | —           |
| **Total**                        | **₱504.32**      | **₱201.66** |

**The post-mortem fee is a worked example, not a flat rate.** For cattle and
carabao the charter computes it as 300 kg × 80% × 0.693 = ₱166.32, plus ₱60.
For swine it shows 70 × 90% × 1.563 = ₱98.46, with no unit given for the 70;
that multiplication actually comes to ₱98.469, so ₱98.47 rounded.

These are the amounts the charter states. The ordinance that sets them has not
been retrieved.

## Renewing a butcher's permit

The permit, license and health certificate show that a working butcher is
disease-free.

### What to bring

| Document                            | Where to get it                |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Slaughterhouse clearance            | Market & Slaughterhouse Office |
| Notarised butcher's contract        | Public Attorney's Office       |
| Barangay clearance                  | Barangay hall                  |
| Community Tax Certificate (cedula)  | Municipal Treasurer's Office   |
| Police clearance                    | Police station                 |
| Blood test, including HBsAg         | Divine Ray's Diagnostic Center |
| Drug test                           | Divine Ray's Diagnostic Center |
| Health certificate                  | Municipal Sanitary Office      |
| Personal protective equipment (PPE) | Your own                       |

The diagnostic center is the one the charter names.

### Steps

| You                                                                         | The office                                              | Time      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Ask the meat inspector for renewal and the list of requirements          | Gives you the list, the clearance and the contract      | 1 minute  |
| 2. Pay the fees to the revenue collecting clerk                             | Collects the payment                                    | 3 minutes |
| 3. Take the receipt and your HBsAg result to the Health and Sanitary Office | The sanitation inspector issues your health certificate | 2 minutes |
| 4. Hand all your requirements to the meat inspector                         | Checks and approves them                                | 1 minute  |

Total: about **7 minutes**, once you have the documents.

### Fees

| Fee                            | Amount      |
| ------------------------------ | ----------- |
| Mayor's permit                 | ₱250.00     |
| Police clearance               | ₱50.00      |
| Documentary stamp              | ₱30.00      |
| Authentication fee             | ₱10.00      |
| Sanitary permit and inspection | ₱200.00     |
| Health certificate             | ₱50.00      |
| Occupation fee                 | ₱100.00     |
| Secretary's fee                | ₱50.00      |
| Cedula                         | ₱200.00     |
| **Total**                      | **₱940.00** |

The blood test and drug test are paid separately at the diagnostic center.
