# Get free rice seeds, fertilizer and vegetable seedlings

The Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) gives out **rice seeds and fertilizer**
to RSBSA-registered farmers, together with help applying for crop insurance,
and **vegetable seedlings** to farmers who have prepared a planting area. Both
are **free**. Rice distribution takes about **2 hours and 25 minutes** on the
day; seedlings about **1 hour and 45 minutes**. The charter lists no
distribution of fingerlings.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Rice seeds and fertilizer

Run by the MAO in partnership with the Department of Agriculture (DA).

**Who can get them:** farmers registered in the **RSBSA**. On the day, the MAO
checks your record against the DA's Farm & Fishery Registry System (FFRS).

**The rice straw burning rule.** The MAO checks whether you have violated
Municipal Ordinance No. 2019-087, against rice straw burning.

- If you have a violation, bring the **official receipt** showing you paid the
  penalty.
- With **more than three offenses**, you cannot receive the seeds and
  fertilizer.

### What to bring

- An **original valid ID** and a **photocopy** of it
- The payment receipt, if you have a straw-burning violation

### Steps

| You                                                                         | The office                            | Time       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Go to the MAO on the distribution date your Punong Barangay announces    | Checks your record against the FFRS   | 20 minutes |
| 2. If you have a straw-burning violation, hand in the payment receipt       | Records it                            | 15 minutes |
| 3. Attend the technical briefing on rice crop management                    | Gives the briefing                    | 30 minutes |
| 4. Sign the acknowledgement receipt                                         | Prepares it                           | 30 minutes |
| 5. Fill out the crop insurance (PCIC) application and the CSM feedback form | Hands out the forms and collects them | 20 minutes |
| 6. Receive your seeds and fertilizer                                        | Distributes them                      | 30 minutes |

Total: about **2 hours and 25 minutes**. No fees.

The insurance application in step 5 goes to the Philippine Crop Insurance
Corporation (PCIC). For insurance outside a distribution day, and for filing a
claim, see
[Get help with crop pests, soil testing and insurance](/services/agriculture-fisheries/get-help-with-crop-pests-soil-testing-and-insurance).

## Vegetable seedlings

**Who can get them:** farmers, farmers' associations and cooperatives from
Carigara.

### What to bring

- A **valid ID**
- Your **RSBSA reference number** — the MAO can give it to you
- A **picture of your planting area**, already thoroughly prepared

### Steps

| You                                                                                                                                                | The office                                           | Time       |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Go to the MAO during the scheduled distribution, show the picture of your prepared area, and attend a short orientation on vegetable production | Checks the area to be planted and gives the briefing | 1 hour     |
| 2. Sign the acknowledgement receipt and fill out the CSM feedback form                                                                             | Prepares the forms                                   | 15 minutes |
| 3. Receive the seedlings                                                                                                                           | Distributes them                                     | 30 minutes |

Total: about **1 hour and 45 minutes**. No fees.

## Fingerlings

The charter does not list any distribution of fingerlings, or any other
service for fishers besides fisheries insurance.
