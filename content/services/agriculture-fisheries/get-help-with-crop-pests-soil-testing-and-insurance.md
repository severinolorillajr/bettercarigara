# Get help with crop pests, soil testing and insurance

The Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) offers three **free** services to
farmers: a **field visit** when pests or disease hit your crops (about **2½
hours**), a **soil test** with a fertilizer recommendation (about **7 days**),
and help applying for **crop, livestock or fisheries insurance** with the
Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC). If you are insured and suffer a
loss, report it to the MAO **within 3 working days**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

**Who can use these services:** farmers, and farmers' associations and
cooperatives, from Carigara. The MAO has separate staff for rice, for corn and
cassava, and for high-value crops; for insurance, also for livestock and for
fisheries.

## Crop pests and diseases

**Bring:** a valid ID.

| You                                                                   | The office                                                         | Time                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| 1. Go to the MAO and ask for help identifying or managing the problem | Asks about the outbreak and schedules a visit to the affected area | 30 minutes                         |
| 2. Walk the affected area with the agricultural extension worker      | Inspects the crops and recommends treatment and control            | 2 hours, depending on the distance |

Total: about **2 hours and 30 minutes**. No fees.

## Soil analysis and fertilizer recommendation

The MAO tests your soil with a Soil Test Kit and tells you **what fertilizer
to use, how much, and when**.

**Bring:** a valid ID. You can visit in person or send a letter of request.

| You                                                                             | The office                                                               | Time                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| 1. Visit the MAO or send a letter of request                                    | Asks what you will plant, where the farm is, and when to collect samples | 30 minutes                         |
| 2. On the agreed date, walk the farm with the agricultural extension worker     | Shows you how to collect soil samples, and geotags the area              | 3 hours, depending on the distance |
| 3. **Air-dry the samples for at least 3 to 4 days**, then bring them to the MAO | Analyses them and works out a fertilizer recommendation                  | **7 days**                         |
| 4. Collect the result                                                           | Gives you a printed copy and goes through the recommendation with you    | 30 minutes                         |

No fees. The charter gives the total as **7 days, 3 hours and 30 minutes**, but
its steps add up to 7 days and 4 hours — and that is before the 3 to 4 days of
drying.

## Crop, livestock and fisheries insurance

The MAO helps you apply to the **PCIC**, which covers losses from pests,
disease and natural calamities.

**Bring:**

- A **valid ID**
- Your **RSBSA reference number** — the MAO can give it to you

| You                                                                                              | The office                                                       | Time       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Get the PCIC application and CSM feedback forms from the MAO, fill them out, and hand them in | Checks the application and that all requirements are there       | 30 minutes |
| 2. **Keep a photocopy** of your filled-out application, in case it is lost or for follow-up      | Prepares a summary report and sends the applications to the PCIC | 7 days     |
| 3. **If you suffer a loss**, hand in a Notice of Loss at the MAO **within 3 working days**       | Sends it to the PCIC                                             | 12 hours   |

No fees to the MAO. Rice farmers can also fill out the PCIC application on a
seed distribution day; see
[Get free rice seeds, fertilizer and vegetable seedlings](/services/agriculture-fisheries/ask-for-free-seeds-seedlings-fertilizers-or-fingerlings).
