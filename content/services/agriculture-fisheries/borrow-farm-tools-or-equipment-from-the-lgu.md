# Rent a farm tractor from the Municipal Agriculture Office

The Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) rents out a farm tractor, with an
operator, for land preparation. It costs **₱750 per hour**, paid at the
Treasurer's Office in advance. After an inspection of your field, you join a
**first come, first served** queue; the charter allows **20 days** for your
turn.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

**Who can rent:** farmers, and farmers' associations and cooperatives, from
Carigara.

## What to bring

- A **valid ID**
- Later, the **official receipt** of your payment from the Treasurer's Office

## Steps

| You                                                                                 | The office                                                                                  | Time                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 1. Go to the MAO and fill out the rental form with details of the area to be plowed | Explains the operating guidelines and sets a date to inspect your field                     | 30 minutes                         |
| 2. Show the field inspector the area                                                | Assesses its size, terrain, hazards and access route, and estimates how many hours it needs | 2 hours, depending on the distance |
| 3. Pay the rental at the Treasurer's Office                                         | Issues an official receipt                                                                  | 10 minutes                         |
| 4. Hand the receipt to the MAO and wait for your turn                               | Records you and gives you a schedule                                                        | **20 days**                        |

Total, by the charter's count: **20 days, 2 hours and 40 minutes**.

## Fee

| Fee            | Amount               |
| -------------- | -------------------- |
| Tractor rental | **₱750.00 per hour** |

The number of hours comes from the inspector's estimate in step 2. The
charter's own total for this service says "None" for fees, although step 3
charges ₱750 an hour.
