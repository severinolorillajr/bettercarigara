# Get permission to cut trees

If you want to cut trees you planted on your property, the municipality can
issue a **certificate of no objection** — its confirmation that LGU Carigara
does not object, subject to national environmental laws and rules. Bring a
barangay certification. It is **free** and takes about **8 minutes** at the
Municipal Administrator's Office.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

---

## Where

|         |                                                                        |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Office  | Office of the Municipal Administrator                                  |
| Address | 2nd floor, Municipal Hall, E. Makabenta St., Barangay Ponong, Carigara |
| Phone   | (053) 888 2903                                                         |
| Email   | carigaramunicipality@gmail.com                                         |
| Who     | Property owners, or their authorized representative                    |

**This is not the only permission you may need.** The certificate is "subject
to compliance with the existing environmental laws, rules, and regulations".
The charter does not say what those require or which national office issues
them.

## What to bring

- A **barangay certification** from the barangay where you live, confirming
  that you are a bona fide resident, that you own the trees to be cut, and that
  the barangay has no objection

## Steps

| You                                   | The office                                                                          | Time      |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Present the barangay certification | The Municipal Administrator reviews it and any supporting documents                 | 3 minutes |
| 2. Wait for the certificate           | Prepares the certificate, signed by the Mayor or the Administrator, and releases it | 5 minutes |

## Fees

| Fee                         | Amount   |
| --------------------------- | -------- |
| Certificate of no objection | **None** |
