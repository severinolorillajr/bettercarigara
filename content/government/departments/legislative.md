# Sangguniang Bayan

The **Sangguniang Bayan** is the legislative body of the Municipality of
Carigara. It passes ordinances and resolutions, approves the municipal budget,
and exercises oversight over the executive branch.

It is presided over by the **Vice Mayor**, who votes only to break a tie.

---

The Sangguniang Bayan also seats **ex-officio members** — the president of the
Liga ng mga Barangay and the president of the Sangguniang Kabataan federation.
Those two seats are not filled by the municipal election and are not listed
above, because this site has not verified who currently holds them.
