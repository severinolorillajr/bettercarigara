# Municipal offices and how to reach them

Most municipal offices are in the **Municipal Hall** on E. Makabenta St.,
Barangay Ponong — the **ground floor** for the Treasurer, Assessor, Civil
Registrar, business permits and social welfare, the **2nd floor** for the
Mayor and most other offices. The Sangguniang Bayan has its own Legislative
Building, and disaster response and the library are on Aguja St., Barangay
Baybay. **Opening hours are not published.**

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Feedback and complaints

The **Public Assistance and Complaint Desk (PACD)** at the Municipal Hall takes
feedback and complaints for all offices — email carigaralgu@yahoo.com or call
or text 0912 460 9390. See
[how to give feedback or file a complaint](/government/departments/feedback-and-complaints).
