# Get documents from, and file matters with, the Sangguniang Bayan

The Sangguniang Bayan (municipal council) office gives residents copies of its
documents and **certified true copies of ordinances and resolutions** for
**₱150 per page**, accredits **civil society organizations (CSOs)** for free in
about **2 days** plus committee hearings and sessions, and receives
**complaints** that need council action, which it must act on **within 10
working days**. Every request starts with a **letter**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Certified true copies of ordinances or resolutions

For anyone — individuals, businesses or offices.

**What to bring**

- A **request letter** addressed to the SB with your name, address, contact
  number and the purpose of the request
- A **virus-free USB storage device** — you also get a digital copy

| You                                    | The office                                                     | Time       |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Request the ordinance or resolution | Searches the logbook and records section                       | 10 minutes |
| 2. Pay at the Treasurer's Office       | Issues an official receipt                                     | —          |
| 3. Show the receipt at the SB office   | Checks it                                                      | 10 minutes |
| 4. Collect                             | Prints the document and copies the electronic file to your USB | 10 minutes |

| Fee                 | Amount               |
| ------------------- | -------------------- |
| Certified true copy | **₱150.00 per page** |

The charter's total says **20 minutes**, but its steps add up to **30
minutes**.

## Copies of other SB documents

For the general public, for any legal purpose.

**What to bring:** a **request letter** to the Sangguniang Bayan saying which
document you need.

| You                                  | The office                       | Time       |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Hand in your request letter       | Records it                       | 5 minutes  |
|                                      | Prepares a copy of the document  | 15 minutes |
|                                      | Gives you a tax order of payment | 5 minutes  |
| 2. Pay at the Treasurer's Office     | Issues an official receipt       | —          |
| 3. Show the receipt at the SB office | Releases the documents           | 10 minutes |

| Fee  | Amount               |
| ---- | -------------------- |
| Copy | **₱150.00 per page** |

Charter total: **35 minutes**.

## Certified copies of official documents

For anyone who needs supporting official documents for employment, business,
or access to local or national services.

**What to bring:** a **request letter** addressed to the Sangguniang Bayan with
your name, address, contact number and the purpose of the request, plus any
supporting documents.

| You                                                                                 | The office                                      | Time       |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Hand in your documents, letters, notices, endorsements, referrals or invitations | Reviews them and stamps the date and time       | 10 minutes |
| 2. Pay at the Treasurer's Office                                                    | Issues an official receipt                      | —          |
| 3. Show the receipt at the SB office                                                | Checks it                                       | 10 minutes |
|                                                                                     | Forwards it to SB staff to schedule and endorse | 30 minutes |
|                                                                                     | Tells you what action was taken                 | 60 minutes |

| Fee  | Amount               |
| ---- | -------------------- |
| Copy | **₱150.00 per page** |

The charter's total says **1 hour 40 minutes**, but its steps add up to **1
hour 50 minutes**. Its steps also describe the SB _receiving_ correspondence
from outside the municipality rather than issuing certified copies, so ask at
the office which applies to you.

## Accreditation of a civil society organization (CSO)

Accreditation recognizes your organization and lets it become a partner of
the municipality, through representation in a local special body.

**What to bring**

| Document                                  | Where to get it   |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Letter of intent to the Sangguniang Bayan | Your organization |
| By-laws                                   | Your organization |
| SEC certificate                           | SEC               |
| List of officers and members              | Your organization |
| Annual accomplishment report              | Your organization |
| Annual financial statement                | Your organization |
| Board resolution seeking accreditation    | Your organization |
| Endorsement from the barangay             | Your barangay     |

| You                                         | The council                                           | Time                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| 1. Hand in your letter and documents        | Receives them and forwards them to the SB Secretary   | 5 minutes              |
|                                             | SB Secretary puts it on the members' agenda           | 30 minutes             |
|                                             | Refers it to the committee concerned                  | At a scheduled session |
|                                             | Prepares the referral and notices of hearing          | 15 minutes             |
|                                             | Committee holds hearings                              | As scheduled           |
|                                             | Committee writes its report                           | 30 minutes per report  |
|                                             | Committee presents its recommendation to the council  | At a scheduled session |
|                                             | Drafts and finalizes the resolution                   | 1 day                  |
|                                             | Resolution is signed by the legislative and executive | 1 day                  |
|                                             | Prepares the Certificate of Accreditation             | 15 minutes             |
| 2. Collect the Certificate of Accreditation | Logs and releases it                                  | 10 minutes             |

**Fee: none.** Charter total: **2 days, 1 hour and 45 minutes** — not counting
the time waiting for sessions and hearings, which the charter does not
estimate.

## File a complaint with the council

For individuals, businesses or offices with complaints or other matters that
need action by the council.

**What to bring:** your complaint in **original copy** with any attachments.
You get a receiving copy back.

| You                       | The council                                                                                                                                                                | Time      |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1. Hand in your complaint | Receives it and forwards it to the legislative members                                                                                                                     | 5 minutes |
|                           | The SB member concerned reviews it                                                                                                                                         | 3 hours   |
|                           | Forwards it to the SB Secretary for the agenda, referral to the Committee on Response/Feedback Mechanism, or other action, under Feedback Mechanism Ordinance No. 2025-222 | 24 hours  |

It **must be acted upon within 10 working days** from the date of receipt.
Responsible: the SB Secretary, with the Vice Mayor as chairperson. **Fee:
none.** Charter total: **1 day, 3
hours and 5 minutes**.

For complaints about municipal staff or services in general, see
[how to give feedback or file a complaint](/government/departments/feedback-and-complaints).
