# Request a meeting with the Mayor, or a letter from the Mayor's Office

Anyone — an individual, a group or an organization — can ask for a meeting or
courtesy call with the Mayor by handing in a **letter of request in 2
copies** stating the agenda and objectives. The Mayor's staff call you back
with a date, time and venue in **1–2 days**. The same office issues **free**
job recommendations, referrals of indigent patients for hospital, medical or
financial assistance, and endorsement letters to other agencies, in about **17
minutes**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Request a meeting or courtesy call

**What to bring:** a **letter of request, 2 copies**, with complete
information such as the **agenda and objectives** of the meeting. One copy
stays with the office; the other is stamped and returned to you as your
receiving copy.

| You                                                | The office                                                                   | Time         |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| 1. Hand in both copies of your letter              | Stamps them received, records the date and time, returns your receiving copy | 2 minutes    |
| 2. Explain the purpose of the meeting              | Records the details                                                          | 5 minutes    |
| 3. Wait for the Executive Assistant to contact you | Checks the Mayor's schedule and calls you with the date, venue and time      | **1–2 days** |

**Fee: none.** The charter's total says **12 minutes**; its steps add up to 7
minutes at the counter, plus **1–2 days** waiting for a schedule.

## Get a job recommendation, referral or endorsement letter

For residents and job applicants. The charter lists three kinds of letter:

| Letter                                                                        | What to bring                                                 | Where to get it                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recommendation for employment                                                 | Personal Data Sheet                                           | You fill it out                                         |
| Referral of an indigent patient for hospital, medical or financial assistance | Certificate of Indigency                                      | The barangay captain where you live                     |
|                                                                               | Hospital bill or medical abstract, if the patient is confined | The hospital where the patient is admitted              |
|                                                                               | Case study                                                    | Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO) |
| Endorsement letter to the right agency                                        | Not listed in the charter                                     | —                                                       |

| You                          | The office                                                      | Time       |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1. Sign the logbook          | Takes down your details                                         | 2 minutes  |
| 2. Hand in your requirements | Checks them and prepares the letter                             | 10 minutes |
| 3. Collect your letter       | Dry-seals it and releases it, signed by an authorized signatory | 5 minutes  |

**Fee: none.** Charter total: **17 minutes**.
