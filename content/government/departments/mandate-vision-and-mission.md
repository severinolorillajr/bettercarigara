# Mandate, vision and mission

The municipal government's own statement of what it is for, where it wants
Carigara to go, and what its officials and employees promise residents. The
text below is quoted exactly as the municipality published it in its
**Citizen's Charter 2026**.

---

## Mandate

> To serve services with utmost accountability, responsibility and integrity to
> build people-centered governance, strict adherence to service standards and
> exhibit firm decision and action in achieving the vision for development and
> fulfilling the mission for thriving community.

## Vision

> CARIGARA is center of competitive trade and industry in the Northern part of
> Leyte with empowered and God-loving citizenry enjoying a self-sufficient
> local economy in an ecologically-balanced environment with quality and
> essential support infrastructure under a transparent, accountable,
> responsive, committed, and competent leadership.

## Mission

> The Local Governance of Carigara in partnership with the people shall provide
> development opportunities, ensure public safety and proper management of
> resources, promote programs for economic and political stability and create
> a venue for socio-cultural upliftment towards the attainment of quality of
> life for Kalgaran-ons.

## Service pledge

> We, the officials and employees of the Municipal Government of Carigara,
> pledge and commit to deliver quality public services as promised in the
> Citizens Charter; such as
>
> - **C**ommitment and practice highly observed ethical standards in public
>   governance,
> - **A**ssurance of effective and efficient response to the needs of clients,
> - **R**eliability and consistency in promoting programs for economic growth
>   and stability,
> - **I**mplementing simplified requirements to manage human resource,
> - **G**uiding clients in every transaction to achieve public service with
>   God-loving citizenry,
> - **A**voiding partiality in dealing with clients,
> - **R**esponding complaint about services with highest degree of
>   professionalism, and
> - **A**mity and camaraderie among members.

The first letters of the eight lines spell **CARIGARA**.

The pledge is the standard the charter's services are meant to meet. If a
service falls short of it, see
[Give feedback or file a complaint](/government/departments/feedback-and-complaints).
