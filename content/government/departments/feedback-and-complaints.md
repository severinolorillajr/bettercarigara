# Give feedback or file a complaint

Feedback and complaints about any municipal office go to the **Public
Assistance and Complaint Desk (PACD)** at the Municipal Hall, by **email to
carigaralgu@yahoo.com**, or by **call or text to 0912 460 9390**. Complaints
are evaluated and investigated by the **Municipal Administrator**, who acts as
Complaints Officer. The charter lists no fee. It also lists three national
channels: the Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA), the 8888 hotline and the
CSC Contact Center ng Bayan.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

## Give feedback

1. **Go to the PACD** and look for the personnel-in-charge.
2. **Fill out the form** and hand it to the PACD Officer.
3. The PACD Officer **forwards it to the office concerned**.
4. You are **told what action was taken**.

You can also send feedback by email to carigaralgu@yahoo.com or call
0912 460 9390. Feedback that needs an answer goes to the office concerned, and
its answer is relayed back to you.

### The customer feedback form

After a transaction you may be asked to fill out a **Client Satisfaction
Measurement** form. It is optional, and the charter says personal information
on it is kept confidential. It asks for:

- **About you:** client type (citizen, business, or government), date, sex,
  age, region of residence, the service you used and the office
- **The Citizen's Charter (CC1–CC3):** whether you know what a citizen's
  charter is, whether this office's was easy to see, and whether it helped
- **Nine statements (SQD0–SQD8)**, each rated from strongly agree to strongly
  disagree, or N/A: overall satisfaction, reasonable time, the office followed
  its stated requirements and steps, simple steps, information easy to find,
  reasonable fees, fair to everyone ("walang palakasan"), courteous staff, and
  getting what you needed (or a clear explanation if denied)
- **Suggestions** and your **email address** — both optional

Drop it in the **suggestion box at the PACD** or another designated area.

## File a complaint

1. **Fill out the complaint form** and give it to the PACD Officer or the HRM
   Office. You can also complain **by call or text** to 0912 460 9390.
2. Include the **name of the person you are complaining about**, **what
   happened**, and any **evidence**.
3. For follow-ups, call or text the same number.

### What the complaint form asks for

- Date and time
- Your name, contact number, email address, residence address and office
  address
- The name of the person you are complaining about, and their position or
  office
- Your statement of complaint — the back of the page can be used for more
- Your signature over printed name

### What happens next

| Step | The Complaints Officer (Municipal Administrator)                                               |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Evaluates the complaint                                                                        |
| 2    | Starts an investigation and forwards the complaint to the office concerned for its explanation |
| 3    | Writes a report and submits it to the head of the agency for action                            |
| 4    | Gives you the feedback                                                                         |

The charter gives **no processing time** for complaints at the PACD.

Complaints that need action by the municipal council can also be filed with
the Sangguniang Bayan, which must act on them **within 10 working days** — see
[Sangguniang Bayan services](/government/departments/sangguniang-bayan-services).
