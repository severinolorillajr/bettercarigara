# Request public records (Freedom of Information)

Anyone can ask the municipality for official records, public documents and
government data under Freedom of Information (FOI). Bring a **letter that
clearly describes the information you need** and a **valid ID**. The charter
lists the service twice, and the two versions differ: through the **Mayor's
Office** it takes **15 days**, with "reproduction cost may apply"; through the
**Public Assistance and Complaints Desk** under the Treasurer it takes **10
days**, with listed charges such as **₱50 per certification** and **₱2 per
photocopy**.

Everything below about Carigara comes from the municipality's own **Citizen's
Charter 2026**.

The charter does not say which desk to use for which kind of record, or why
there are two.

**Who can request:** anyone who wants to access information held by the
municipality — individuals and other government agencies.

## What to bring

- An **FOI letter request** that clearly describes the information you need
- A **valid ID** — a company ID or any government-issued ID (1 copy)
- If someone is filing for you: an **authorization letter** from you
  (1 original)
- LGU employees asking for their own personal records: a letter request
  (listed in the Mayor's Office version only)

## Steps — Mayor's Office

| You                                                     | The office                                                                               | Time        |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| 1. Hand in your letter and ID, and fill out the logbook | Stamps it received                                                                       | 2 minutes   |
|                                                         | Evaluates the request                                                                    | 5 minutes   |
|                                                         | Tells you whether it is approved or denied                                               | 2 minutes   |
| 2. If approved, wait for the documents                  | Forwards the request to the office holding the information, retrieves it and releases it | **15 days** |
| If denied                                               | Tells you the grounds and issues a notice                                                | 2 minutes   |

Charter total: **15 days and 9 minutes**. Fee: "reproduction cost may apply" —
no amounts given.

## Steps — PACD, Treasurer's Office

| You                                                     | The office                                                                               | Time        |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| 1. Hand in your letter and ID, and fill out the logbook | Stamps it received                                                                       | 2 minutes   |
|                                                         | Evaluates the request                                                                    | 5 minutes   |
|                                                         | Tells you whether it is approved; if so, sends you to pay                                | 2 minutes   |
| 2. Pay at the Treasurer's Office, Window 2, 3 or 7      | Issues an official receipt                                                               | 5 minutes   |
| 3. Wait to be told when to collect                      | Tells you when the documents will be ready                                               | 2 minutes   |
| 4. Collect your documents                               | Forwards the request to the office holding the information, retrieves it and releases it | **10 days** |
| If denied                                               | Tells you the grounds and issues a notice                                                | 5 minutes   |

Window 3 is the **priority lane** for persons with disabilities, pregnant
women and senior citizens.

Charter total: **10 days and 21 minutes**. That total adds the 5-minute denial
step to the approved path; an approved request adds up to 10 days and 16
minutes.

| Charge (PACD version)             | Amount              |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Certification of official records | ₱50.00              |
| Printing, colored ink             | ₱10.00              |
| Printing, black ink               | ₱3.00               |
| Photocopy                         | ₱2.00               |
| Mail                              | Actual postage rate |

The charter says "reproduction cost may apply" and lists these amounts; it does
not say whether printing and photocopy are per page.

## How the two versions differ

|                               | Mayor's Office            | PACD, Treasurer's Office                         |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Classification                | Simple                    | Complex                                          |
| Handled by                    | Mayor's Office staff      | Public Assistance and Complaints Desk            |
| Time if approved              | **15 days**               | **10 days**                                      |
| Charges                       | "May apply" — none listed | Listed above                                     |
| When you pay                  | Not stated                | After approval, before the records are retrieved |
| LGU employee personal records | Listed                    | Not listed                                       |

Both cite the same legal basis: the 1987 Constitution (Article III, Section 7,
the right to information) and Executive Order No. 2, s. 2016.
