# Your Mayor and Vice Mayor

The **Mayor** is the chief executive of the Municipality of Carigara, elected by
residents at the Philippine National and Local Elections held every three years.
The Mayor implements programs and policies, oversees municipal services, and
represents the municipality at official functions.

The **Vice Mayor** presides over the **Sangguniang Bayan** (Municipal Council)
and assumes the Mayor's duties as Acting Mayor when the Mayor is unable to serve.
